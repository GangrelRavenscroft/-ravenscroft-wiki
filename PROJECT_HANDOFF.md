## Delegated territorial House hierarchy and parallel underworld patronage — authoritative user clarification (17 October 2026)

Across the Ravenscroft universe, the Great/Major/Lesser constitutional structure should be visualized like nested Crusader Kings realms (king/duke/count/baron) or federal/state/county responsibility — these are **analogies**, not extra ranks or literal public sovereignty. A sovereign Great House personally administers some domains, oversees multiple directly bound Major Houses, and can directly supervise one or more Lesser Houses and institutions or enterprises. Each bound Major House answers directly to its Great House, administers some territory or specialist remit itself, and may oversee multiple Lesser Houses. A Lesser House may report directly to its Great House or to its assigned Major. There is no requirement to partition every territory into subordinate Houses, and a Major does not constitutionally rule another Major. All charters, private assets and governmental boundaries remain distinct.

Criminal enterprises are a **parallel organizational hierarchy, not an alternate House rank**. A criminal enterprise may report or owe patronage directly to a Great, Major or Lesser House depending on its own power and agreed relationship; powerful criminal groups may have subordinate smaller criminal organizations (syndicate -> local group -> street gang), each with its own internal chain of command. A local gang generally does not know how far up the concealed chain extends. Levels of knowledge, House membership, Order induction and operational clearance remain distinct. Independence, alliances, rivalry and divided loyalties are possible; do not assume all underworld actors belong to the Order or to the same Great House.

This is a clarified **world-wide model**, not permission to manufacture additional subordinate Houses, subordinate ranks, public territory, named enterprises, exact boundaries, or new Amaru offices. The user specifically said the existing subordinate-House detail is enough; prioritize other House development. Website doctrine: `world/great-house-power-structure.html#delegated-territorial-hierarchy` and `#criminal-patronage-hierarchy`.
