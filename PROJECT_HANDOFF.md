## House Valdieri — approved 2026 foundations

- Historic Italian/Piedmontese Great House. Its civil war ran **1934–1946**. After the defeat and settlement it was degraded from Great House to **Major House from 1946 to 2023**. It recovered **Great-House rank in 2023**. In 2026 it is materially the **weakest Great House**, yet enjoys full formal peer standing.
- Current Head: **Lorenzo Vittorio Valdieri** (born 1962); consort **Dr. Elena Beatrice Montanari Valdieri** (born 1968). Children: **Matteo Lorenzo** (born 1996), industrial engineer and heir; **Giulia Caterina** (born 2004), spouse of **Elias Alexander Ravenscroft**, who in 2026 renounced her and her descendants' Valdieri succession rights; **Chiara Elisabetta** (born 2008), second successor, with mathematics/physics/astronomy interests.
- The extinct **Valdieri di Monferrato** rebel bloodline has **NO surviving descendants**. Genova sided with the rebels and suffered punitive wardship/restitution; Canavese ultimately defended the principal House. Ravenscroft covertly exploited the conflict. The international **Articles of Dynastic Continuity (1953–1956)** arose in the postwar aftermath.
- Principal Great-House net worth: **US$3.5–5 billion (2026)**, excluding separately owned cadet and bound-House wealth. No double-counting.
- Constitutional seat: **Rocca delle Fonti**, near Valdieri, Piedmont. Working/commercial seat: **Palazzo Valdieri**, Milan. Separate cadet seats: **Palazzo di Levante** (Genova), **Villa della Rocchetta** (Canavese).
- Core institutions: **Compagnia Patrimoniale Valdieri**, **Officine Valdieri Group**, **Fondazione Valdieri per il Patrimonio e le Scienze Applicate**, **Archivio della Rocca / Ufficio della Continuità**, **Consorzio Tecnico Valdieri**. The industrial group has approximately **7–11 operating sites**, **2,700–4,000 employees**, and **US$1.2–1.9B annual revenue**. Cadet vehicles are **Compagnia di Levante Valdieri** and **Tecnologie Canavese Valdieri**.
- **Consiglio di Casa Valdieri** has **seven seats**. The established protected-decision rule is **five of seven plus the Head's concurrence**. Do not add subordinate Major Heads as voting seats by default. Functions of **Steward of Assets** and **Warden of Constitutional Continuity** are approved; officeholders are still unnamed.

### 2026 hierarchy: four directly subordinate Major Houses

**Constitutional rule:** “Cadet” denotes **blood relationship**, not an intermediate House rank. Cadets can be Major- or Lesser-ranked. **Each Major reports directly to its sovereign Great House**; one Major cannot be constitutional superior of another Major. Majors carry broad *regional House network* obligations, not governmental control or ownership of every business in a province.

| Direct Major House | Head (locked) | Independently owned wealth (locked) | Primary regional mandate (locked broad scope) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Valdieri di Genova** — blood cadet | **Claudia Renata Valdieri di Genova**, b. 1964 | **$1–2B** | Genoa and western Ligurian relationships |
| **Valdieri di Canavese** — blood cadet | **Tommaso Enrico Valdieri di Canavese**, b. 1967 | **$1.5–2.5B** | Canavese/Ivrea and northern Piedmont |
| **House Roccabruna** — non-blood bound Major | **Dr. Beatrice Ludovica Roccabruna**, b. 1969 | **$500–800M** | Southwest Piedmont; Cuneo/Turin–Cuneo corridor |
| **House Marenesi** — non-blood bound Major | **Giorgio Pietro Marenesi**, b. 1970 | **$350–650M** | Eastern Liguria and La Spezia-facing corridor |

**Never place Marenesi beneath Genova.** Giorgio and Claudia are peers; Genoese real estate or business relationships do not grant Claudia dynastic authority. **Rocca delle Fonti** and the principal **Milan** administrative nodes report directly to the Great House despite nearby regional mandates. A House's constitutional regional assignment is not sovereignty over Italian local government.
