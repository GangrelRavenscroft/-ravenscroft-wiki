# House Amaru — Consejo de Casa and Succession Continuity Rules

**WORKING DEVELOPMENT PROPOSAL — 21 October 2026. NOT LOCKED.** Rafael's actual reigning Headship and Nicolás's Principal Deputy/Designated Successor role **are locked separately** in the [Senior Headship Constitution](amaru-senior-headship-and-succession-charter-2026.md).

## Proposed constitutional function

A serious, limited **Consejo de Casa** would help govern a dispersed House through disciplined counsel and implementation monitoring, **not** through a second sovereign authority. Rafael retains the final say on his reserved powers. Nicolás coordinates agenda, information and follow-through. The council is distinct from the Order's **nine-seat International Council** and the Conclave.

The governing principle would be: **the Head decides reserved matters, regional Heads govern within their charters, and the Consejo makes sure the Head receives complete advice.**

**Recommended core:** five established senior family members, with professional and Major-House participation as required. Core presence does not grant automatic access to every classified criminal or Order issue. **Exact permanent membership and voting procedure remain open.**

## Agenda and recorded decisions

The Consejo would receive periodic risk and capital reviews; cross-Major disputes; common patrimony and trust constraints; succession preparation; external strategic commitments; water, agriculture, trade, heritage and community risks; and appropriately compartmented reports on territorial criminal compliance and tribute. **Quarterly full review** is one reasonable option, not a canon meeting calendar.

A proposed decision record includes: subject, originating chartered authority, affected territory and independently owned interests, advice from affected regional Heads, financial/legal/ethical consequences, recommended options, Rafael's decision or an actually delegated officer's approval, implementation owner, and a recorded dissent/appeal path.

**Consejo advice is nonbinding.** It can ask for more information and record an objection but cannot automatically seize property, overrule a Major Head's established rights, issue general criminal orders, depose the Head or bind the Conclave.

## Procedure for absence and transition (PROPOSAL)

**Temporary absence:** Existing House and professional officers continue ordinary work within their charters. Nicolás coordinates within Rafael's actual delegated warrant. No temporary absence alone makes him Head.

**Lawful vacancy:** Verify the vacancy; authenticate the last lawful successor designation; maintain continuity of archives and subordinate administration; acknowledge Nicolás as designated successor subject to the actual House constitution; formally record the new Headship; notify Major Heads and the Order for separate Conclave representation authentication.

**Permanent incapacity:** Needs especially strong independent evidence and clearly defined lawful authorizers so the Consejo cannot manufacture a removal. The evidentiary standard, authorized signatories, archivists, escrow instruments and appeals remain open and should not be fixed without review.

**Major House Heads:** Their acknowledgment preserves constitutional continuity but should not become an invented vote that freely elects a different Head. A genuine contest over authenticity or incapacity may escalate to existing House and Order adjudication.

| Major House Head | Chartered local/regional authority, including supervision of submitted local criminal vassals | Senior House recognition, subordinate personnel and legitimacy | Power over peer Majors or exemption from Great-House territorial supremacy |
| Submitted criminal organization | Daily enterprise operations and subsidiary management | Compliance with territorial House, tribute arrangements and local networks | Independent right to reject House supremacy |

## Explicitly unresolved approvals

Council's formal existence, admission/recusal, quorum and voting method, cycle of meetings, dispute review routes, succession instruments and evidence of incapacity, scope of Nicolás's emergency acting warrant, and possible additional professional offices. **No newly named subordinate House, cartel, assassination, vote, estate ownership or historical crisis is created by this draft.**
