## 1. Executive identity — Amaru as a distributed enduring power

Amaru should not resemble a monolithic utility conglomerate, a modern ministry or a romanticized Inca monarchy. Its distinctive institutional genius would be **continuity across difficult geography and abrupt political change**: adapting capital structures, skilled administrators, regional relationships, transport and water expertise as governments and public property rules change. Its power comes from long-lived institutional memory, discreet private capital and capable local partners.

**Already established:** independently recognized Great House; Peru–Bolivia Andean sphere; senior patriarch **Rafael Esteban Amaru**; senior matriarch **Lucía Marisol Quispe-Amaru**, with a distinguished independent allied lineage; children **Nicolás Rafael** (eldest/principal heir), **Alejandro Mateo** (second son, current external representative/Ravenscroft liaison and Order Tier II) and **Camila Inés** (cultural/philanthropic); Alejandro's marriage to **Élodie Catherine de Villiers** on **12 July 1997** and existing children; separate House Conclave Voice without an assigned International Council of Nine mandate.

**Proposed operating premise:** a sovereign senior household sets policy and enforces chartered limits; directly administered properties, trusts, professional institutions and enterprises coexist with Major House regional mandates and Lesser House local/specialist jurisdictions. Each subordinate House has its own leadership and holdings. Neither a map shaded in Amaru colors nor a chartered superior implies that the House owns a modern country, municipal government, water basin, public road or independently held subordinate assets.

### Proposal: Consejo de Casa

A small **Consejo de Casa** could advise the Head and organize the otherwise disconnected network of the senior household, professional stewards and regional House Heads. **Consejo** is a working name; neither its existence, voting size, specific memberships nor powers are locked. Do **not** confuse it with the Order's elected **International Council of Nine** or the **Conclave of Great-House Voices**.

**Routine decision path (proposal):**
1. A regional matter begins with the responsible Major, Lesser or directly administered unit. Local authorities decide within their charters.
2. A cross-region, reputational, ownership or security issue is escalated to the appropriate senior coordinator, with separate legal and classified reporting as warranted.
3. The senior household receives alternatives, financial exposure and the views of affected subordinate Heads. The council advises rather than replacing local management.
4. The Head or a properly delegated authority makes the reserved decision. Written rationale and obligations move downward through the chartered superior, not arbitrary bypasses.
5. A dissenting subordinate may use a defined internal review or appeal channel. Severe Order-wide Compact issues follow **Order jurisdiction**, not a private Amaru override.

**Suggested reserved matters for later charter review:** Great-House succession recognition, charter creation/revision, binding inter-House commitments, irreversible disposition of genuinely common-House patrimony, exceptional use of collective security resources, and disputes between peer Major Houses. Nonreserved operating decisions stay local. The Head should not be required to approve every procurement, warehouse contract or community grant.

**Suggested cadence for review rather than lock:** recurring regional intelligence and capital exposure reports; a periodic senior review of inter-region conflicts and common risk; an annual continuity exercise. Avoid fictional precision (e.g., claiming an approved monthly schedule, unanimous-vote requirement or named committee) until explicit ratification.

### Succession conflict worth developing

Nicolás knows the physical and institutional inheritance; Alejandro understands the international relationships and may be better known among foreign peers; Camila and Lucía can challenge choices that protect capital while imposing burdens on communities. These are potential **legitimate policy differences**, not canon evidence that the siblings distrust one another, nor a basis for assigning any of them a coup, crime or forbidden succession claim.

An Amaru leadership transition should require more than a death certificate: verify successor legitimacy, protect confidential records, reassure regional Heads and counterparties, preserve access to institutions, and confirm the Order's separate recognition procedures. **No precise succession date, incumbent title or extraordinary event is invented.**

### Direct domains versus delegated jurisdictions

- The senior House may administer the ancestral-estate complex, a Lima institutional node, common records and legitimately common trusts directly—**all particular site and ownership claims still proposed**.
- Each bound Major House may directly administer its own important area or technical mandate while assigning other localities to multiple Lesser Houses. A Lesser may alternatively be directly attached to the Great House.
- Administrative jurisdiction does not transfer subordinate beneficial wealth upward, make public administrators vassals or supersede national law.
- Legitimate firms, patronage systems, intelligence channels and criminal enterprises are **parallel networks**. Criminal organizations can be attached at any House level and can themselves supervise smaller groups; bottom-tier actors may know nothing of the superior House or Order. No criminal group is invented or attached to Amaru merely because this possibility exists.

### Proposed relationship between senior authority and criminal-affiliate supervision

The **ultimate supremacy over bound enterprises is world-wide canon**; Amaru-specific internal delegations are **still proposed**. One credible arrangement would let directly attached senior-House contacts, regional Major/lesser authorities, and affiliated syndicates each manage ordinary relationships while exceptional disputes and threats to central House interests can be referred upward. Nicolás's work as the proposed future coordinating heir might focus on obligations and institutional consequences, but he **does not have an approved criminal enforcement office**. Rafael's own formal Headship and intervention mandate remain likewise unratified. The narrative should preserve the distinction between authority that exists at the apex and what any individual knows or personally directs.

**Sample capital governance:**
- Common-House reserves secure continuity obligations and strategic discretion but are not a private checking account for the patriarch.

- Disputed public water allocations, environmental liabilities, community restitution and noncompliant extraction practices create genuine risks and potential costs.
- Order ties, political access or clandestine leverage are neither cash-equivalent wealth nor unencumbered assets.

### Criminal vassalage and illicit influence — applying the locked world-wide doctrine

**Established world principle:** Criminal organizations truly **bound** to a Great House are semi-autonomous vassals. They manage internal affairs and may supervise their own criminal subordinates, while ultimate jurisdiction and reserved enforcement authority reside with the Great House. A large enterprise can be directly attached to Amaru, a regional Major House, a Lesser House, or a more powerful underworld organization; none thereby becomes a constitutional House. Bound groups owe tribute or other agreed underworld obligations upward through the chain, each intermediate superior retaining a share. Do not treat these transfers as public-government tax authority or count the same revenue multiple times.

The Great Houses have substantially more money, intelligence, security, institutional resources and personnel than most criminal affiliates. A subordinate group's defiance may prompt loss of House protection, removal of leadership, compelled restructuring or — in the ruthless politics of this fictional universe — targeted killings, hostile pressure involving other loyal criminal organizations, or direct intervention. None of those possibilities establishes a **named historical crime** or makes violence risk-free: it can invite retaliation, public investigation, damage to innocent people, threatened business interests and Order-Compact conflict.

**Amaru-specific point:** credible access to Andean drug-trade networks is part of the user's intended House identity. However, no particular cartel/syndicate, gang, regional subordinate-House criminal mandate, trafficking route, annual tribute share or specific violent reprisal is presently assigned. Access and authority over **bound** groups are not ownership or authority over the entire market. A street-level criminal may have no knowledge that Amaru or the Order exists. Neither illicit market gross turnover nor independent syndicate wealth automatically constitutes principal Amaru net beneficial wealth.

References: [Criminal Vassalage and Tribute](../world/house-criminal-vassalage-and-tribute.md) · [Great House criminal authority](../world/great-house-power-structure.html#criminal-vassalage-and-tribute).

### Wealth implications for story

A family may be worth many hundreds of billions while experiencing **short-run cash constraints, minority shareholder challenges, currency exposure, blocked assets, binding concession terms, reputational costs, and conflict between legally distinct ownership vehicles**. An Amaru Head can be immensely powerful without owning or being able to liquidate every institution under the House umbrella.

### Order and Conclave boundary

Recognized Amaru sovereignty carries a **Conclave Voice**. Its **2026 representation on the elected Nine International Council is unassigned**: do not assume one of the nine seats or invent a Council vote. An Amaru sovereign Head cannot automatically command members of other Great Houses or subordinate families chartered to them. Blood, marriage, patronage, capital, constitutional standing, Order rank and classified access are different axes of authority.

### Friction that does not require invented wars or villains

- A legitimate water or environmental agreement threatens a valuable Amaru contractor; senior family and local Major differ on response.
- An Amaru-led engineering project relies on logistics expertise from a peer House without granting either House governing authority over the other.
- An inter-House financial alliance yields influence but exposes senior House investments to regional counterpart failure.
- Alejandro negotiates internationally while Nicolás protects the domestic House's future; neither role supersedes the other.
- An allied Quispe obligation imposes competing ethical and dynastic claims; Lucía's influence matters without formal office.
