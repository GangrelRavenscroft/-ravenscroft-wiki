# House Amaru — Senior Headship and Succession Constitution (2026)

**PARTS I–III: CANON established 21 October 2026, continuing the user's approved leadership plan.** This is the date of the editorial record, not an invented date of an actual inauguration. **PARTS IV–V: procedural working proposals, not locked.** [Consejo draft](amaru-consejo-de-casa-and-continuity-2026-proposal.md).

## I. Rafael Esteban Amaru — reigning Head (CANON)

**Rafael Esteban Amaru is the reigning constitutional Head of sovereign Great House Amaru in 2026.** He was already the recognized senior patriarch. This status formalizes the House leadership direction; it does not give him a title in the public governments of Peru or Bolivia, or personal ownership of assets belonging to his Major or Lesser Houses.

Rafael retains final reserved House authority over:
- Designation and constitutionally valid recognition of the successor;
- Material amendment, recognition and adjudication of subordinate House charters, consistent with the actual established rights of their Heads;
- Binding commitments between sovereign Great Houses, accreditation of Amaru's Conclave Delegate or Alternate, and extraordinary House obligations;
- Decisions affecting **genuinely common-House beneficial property** (not all trust, subordinate, client or affiliated property);
- Disputes between peer Major Houses and significant senior territorial matters;
- Ultimate territorial criminal supremacy and tribute within recognized Amaru jurisdiction, following the **universal Great-House doctrine**. This does not imply personally commanding every syndicate or approving any named crime.

Ordinary operation remains delegated through regional Houses, genuinely empowered professional officers, lawful trustees, and the semi-autonomous criminal hierarchies where they have submitted.

## II. Nicolás Rafael Amaru — Principal Deputy and Designated Successor (CANON)

**Nicolás Rafael Amaru**, Rafael's eldest son and established principal heir, serves as **Principal Deputy of House Amaru** and **Designated Successor** to the senior Headship.

His **current, active** responsibilities encompass senior-family continuity, consolidated House risk and finance briefings, administrative coordination among senior domains and relevant Major Houses, implementation of actual delegated decisions, and preparation of succession records. He is a serious administrator rather than an heir who merely waits.

**Limits:** He is not co-Head, does not automatically exercise the House's Conclave Voice, does not own subordinate properties, does not outrank the Head, does not independently govern each Major House, and has no universal operational command of organized-crime enterprises. His mandate depends on formal delegation and preserves Major and Lesser charters. Temporary emergency powers and the exact process of activation upon a vacancy are still to be ratified.

**Succession standing:** Nicolás is the first designated successor. No actual death, incapacity, abdication, coup, ceremony, accession or unrecorded succession date is created.

## III. Preserved family and Order status (CANON)

**Lucía Marisol Quispe-Amaru** remains senior matriarch with substantial influence through the allied Quispe lineage; her influence does not invent a separate House rank or Office of State. **Alejandro Mateo Amaru** remains Senior External Representative, Principal Ravenscroft–Amaru Inter-House Liaison, Order Tier II, and spouse of Élodie Catherine de Villiers; these actual external responsibilities coexist with Nicolás's deputyship. **Camila Inés Amaru** has established cultural preservation and philanthropic interests, without an invented ministry or personal Order office.

Amaru is an independent Great House with **one Conclave Voice**, exercised by its Head or an eligible accredited delegate. Its 2026 elected Council of Nine mandate remains unassigned; the Nine are seats, not the number of Great Houses. Blood, membership, professional authority and Order clearance are separate. The accepted financial bands remain **US$350–650B principal beneficial net worth and US$1–2T broader nonadditive influence**.

## IV. Proposed Consejo de Casa (NOT LOCKED)

A small family-centered **Consejo de Casa** would advise the Head on common risks, major regional disputes, strategic finance, continuity and inter-House affairs. The recommended core comprises Rafael as chair, Nicolás as strategic coordinator and Lucía, Alejandro and Camila as senior family participants, with relevant professional stewards and affected Major House Heads invited for appropriate matters. It has **no independent veto** against Rafael, does not replace local House Heads and is not the International Council. Membership, quorum, appointment powers, voting rules and schedule are open. See [working rules](amaru-consejo-de-casa-and-continuity-2026-proposal.md).

## V. Proposed accession and incapacity procedure (NOT LOCKED)

Recommended continuity: verify an actual lawful vacancy separately from temporary absence; preserve existing delegated operations; authenticate the last valid succession instrument; recognize Nicolás's designated standing; record the lawful transition; notify regional Heads; separately authenticate the House Head or delegate under existing Order rules. Temporary absence would require existing authority or a valid acting warrant and **should not permit a council to declare Rafael removed**.

**Open:** legal/medical verification of permanent incapacity, who holds succession records, witness/authentication requirements, challenge procedure, interim signing limits, ceremonial practices and any fixed reporting timetable.
