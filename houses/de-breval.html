Recognized nonblood bound Major • 2026

Where industrial Europe crosses borders.

A Belgian-born dynasty bound by charter directly to Great House de Villiers, de Bréval maintains its own Headship, succession, beneficial assets and institutions. It coordinates House-affiliated infrastructure and commercial relations across Belgium, with a particularly dense historical presence in Wallonia and Brussels and individually negotiated ties to Flanders.

01

House Identity

Locked October 2026

De Bréval is a distinct Belgian blood family, not de Villiers by blood. Its current expertise spans rail and freight systems, industrial renewal, engineering projects, water/building infrastructure and cross-border infrastructure negotiation. Geneviève favors documentary precision and real technical understanding of Belgian multilingual commercial conditions. The de Villiers parent House possesses reserved constitutional authority under its House charter; it does not automatically own de Bréval property or govern Belgium.

Professional counterpart ties in Dutch-speaking Belgium are negotiated, not imposed. Belgian public institutions, the port of Antwerp, Brussels international organizations and independent companies are not de Bréval state property or subjects.

05

Constitutional Relations and External Counterparts

Geneviève answers directly to sovereign Head Philippe Étienne de Villiers under the Major House charter. She does not occupy one of the two blood-cadet seats on the seven-seat senior Conseil by right of being a Major Head. Consultation and appeals are to be formalized. Joint ventures with northern French House d'Aubrecourt are conducted through reciprocal contracts; neither bound Major is sovereign over the other.

De Bréval also interfaces with the Swiss House de Corvelles, through discreet cross-border engineering, investment and technical compliance work. Corporate investments, other Great-House affiliations, financial valuations and House security protocols remain intentionally open.
