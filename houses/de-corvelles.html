Engineering resilience amid the Alps.

House de Corvelles is a Swiss-born dynasty bound directly to Great House de Villiers, with a protected but restricted remit over its affiliated families and technical networks in French-speaking Switzerland. Its engineers, capital advisers and House officers work within independent Swiss laws, cantonal responsibilities and a complicated international marketplace.

01

House Identity and Jurisdiction

Locked October 2026

De Corvelles is a genuine Swiss family with its own ancestry, House charter, estate and staff. It is not a de Villiers blood cadet and is not simply a French family expatriated to Geneva. The House coordinates de Villiers-affiliated matters in **Geneva, Vaud, Neuchâtel, Fribourg and appropriate French-speaking portions of Valais**; it holds no general dynastic authority in Zürich, Basel, Ticino or the rest of Switzerland absent individually negotiated relationships.

Its specialties are hydraulic systems, hydropower, tunnels and slope hazards, grid performance, engineering reliability and cross-border project investment. It does not own the cantons, public dams, independent Swiss banks, Geneva international bodies or public authorities. Legal corporate relationships must be separately established.

05

Relations and Real Authority

Étienne answers directly to Great House Head Philippe Étienne de Villiers, not through the French Alpine House Montferrand. A joint hydropower or tunnel program can assign a technical project leader without making one Major subordinate to the other. De Corvelles also negotiates with the Belgian House de Bréval on individually agreed capital or engineering assignments.

Neither Étienne nor Geneviève automatically becomes a voting blood-cadet member of the parent seven-seat Conseil; consultation, cross-border arbitration and protected House charters are being separately developed. Swiss technical professions and independent public authorities retain their legal rights and duties.
