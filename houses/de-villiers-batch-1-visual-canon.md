## Locked visual scope

- **House heraldry:** deep burgundy field, ivory/silver fortified stone gatehouse with one entrance and antique-gold surveyor's dividers above, as shown in the approved images. The full achievement includes its specific supporters, coronet and ornament; the simplified seal uses the approved corresponding shield. **No bridge, waves, Valdieri-style blue shield, or eight-point star.**
- **Château de Montrevaux:** approved visual exterior of the ancestral and constitutional Burgundian seat.
- **Hôtel de Villiers:** approved exterior of the separate Paris institutional/administrative headquarters.
- **Montrevaux Library & Archives:** approved interior image of the Château's private library and archival collection.
- **Paris Institutional Council Room:** approved institutional conference-room image at Hôtel de Villiers.
- **No House motto:** “The Architects of Continuity” is the House epithet, not an approved motto or shield inscription.
