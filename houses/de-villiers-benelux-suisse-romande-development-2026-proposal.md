## A. House de Bréval — Belgian bound Major

### Proposed identity and scope
House de Bréval is a real **independent Belgian blood lineage** that chooses chartered allegiance to de Villiers, rather than a de Villiers blood cadet. Its center of gravity is **Wallonia** (historic industry and water, rail/river crossings) and **Brussels** (project contracting, cross-border coordination, arbitration, professional diplomacy). Flanders, including Antwerp/Ghent, hosts selective counterparties but is not claimed as a captive House dominion. Within the hidden Order de Bréval has regional jurisdiction over de Villiers-chartered lesser families, protective liaison and regional obligations in Belgium; **Belgium is not a political province owned by de Villiers**.

### Proposed contemporary family
- **Head:** **Geneviève Thérèse de Bréval** — born **8 April 1967**, Belgian, age 59 in 2026. Trained as a civil/industrial engineer, later developed board and public-private contract risk expertise. Native French, fluent Dutch and English. Exact university and credentials open. Direct, methodical, exceptionally comfortable with multilingual Belgian negotiation and the real costs of cross-border industrial decisions.
- **Spouse:** **Pieter Willem van den Broeck** — born **23 November 1964**, Belgian of a separately successful Dutch-speaking commercial family. Professionally works in logistics, industrial risk and export services, rather than merely being the Head's consort. Is **married-in**; original patronage, wealth, Order standing and clearance open.
- **Children, all proposed:** **Louise Geneviève de Bréval**, b. **16 February 1994**, infrastructure policy and finance; **Antoine Pieter de Bréval**, b. **30 September 1997**, industrial automation/maintenance; **Mathilde Claire de Bréval**, b. **17 June 2002**, graduate civil engineering and water systems. No heir automatically confirmed; eldest Louise is a plausible working first successor subject to charter.
- **Proposed principal seat:** **Domaine de la Houblonnière**, an explicitly fictional historic working estate outside Namur, with private archival and family functions.
- **Proposed professional office:** **Maison de Bréval, Brussels**, practical engineering, legal, financial and liaison staff — not a national governmental HQ.
- **Enterprise niches:** independently held technical concessions and firm shares in rail infrastructure services, building controls, water systems, industrial renewal and professional project finance. No public Belgian utility ownership assumed without a reviewed legal position.

### Distinctive political tensions
She prizes **regional Belgian autonomy** and rejects any implication that Paris can unilaterally pledge de Bréval assets. Her marriage creates practical francophone/Flemish family access without granting domination over either community. De Bréval expects Philippe to consult genuinely before cross-border guarantees and wants notice when d'Aubrecourt's northern French rail teams cross into Belgium. Belgian independent great-power and business counterweights remain real.

## B. House de Corvelles — French-speaking Swiss bound Major

### Proposed identity and scope
A genuinely **Swiss** House with its own descendants, canton-facing relationships, own firms and internal professional heritage. De Corvelles is a **bound nonblood Major**, not a French expatriate branch. Its chartered authority is **de Villiers network affairs in Suisse romande**: Geneva, Vaud, Neuchâtel, Fribourg, and suitable francophone areas of Valais. Federal and cantonal public governments remain independent. Zürich/Basel/Ticino are not automatically within its jurisdiction, though it may co-invest or contract there.

### Proposed contemporary family
- **Head:** **Étienne Marc de Corvelles** — born **19 February 1968**, Swiss, age 58 in 2026. Civil engineer with a second career in project governance, long-term capital and environmental liability. Native French, fluent German and English; conversant Italian. Formal but less hierarchical than Philippe. Business can be arranged carefully with German-speaking Swiss independent partners rather than claimed as natural House territory.
- **Spouse:** **Dr. Léonie Claire Favre de Corvelles** — born **2 June 1970**, Swiss engineering geologist with an active research and consultancy career, trained in hydrogeology and mountain infrastructure. Separately accomplished; her employers and clients are not subsidiaries of her spouse's House.
- **Children, all proposed:** **Juliette Étienne de Corvelles**, b. **24 August 1997**, civil engineering and climate adaptation; **Bastien Marc de Corvelles**, b. **5 December 2000**, risk analytics and secure infrastructure sensing. No successor appointed until branch charter and family choice reviewed.
- **Proposed family seat:** **Maison de Corvelles**, a fictional historic lakeside estate in the Vaud/Lausanne sphere, carefully fitted for modern professional operations; not an actual named Swiss castle assigned to an invented House.
- **Proposed working liaison:** modest **Geneva House Office** in an ordinary legitimate business property, representing House-owned/affiliated interests without authority over international organizations or Swiss banks.
- **Distinct technical niche:** water supply, grid and hydropower modernization, tunnels and slopes, risk underwriting for expensive infrastructure, cross-border construction dispute expertise. No blanket control of Swiss finance.

### Distinctive political tensions
De Corvelles is protective of **Swiss regulatory separation and professional discretion**. The House will not allow French de Villiers officials to treat Geneva as an overseas possession. The Head will collaborate directly with the French Montferrand Major on specific Alpine projects, but **neither Major can command the other**. All such coordination passes through reciprocal project protocols and, in constitutional conflict, the Great House's authorized arbitration channels.

## D. Regional escalation rules — proposal, not final Council charter

- Every Major governs **its own chartered people and House obligations** in its sphere, not residents by nationality.
- Cross-border contract project: appoint a **project lead based on the actual contract**; regional Major Heads keep constitutional authority for their own affiliates. Contract lead is not constitutional superior.
- Inter-Major disputes: direct negotiation → independent professional assessment → authorized Philippe/Conseil constitutional review (final rules still open) → Order Tribunal if external Compact issues arise.
- Mandatory conflict checks for family ownership, professional engineering signoff, major projects with public safety implications, and assets pledged across branches. Safety findings cannot be silenced by patronage alone.
- Professional and political liaisons do not imply counterintelligence clearance. Belgian/Swiss Members, foreign-born spouses and Lesser staff access material on need-to-know criteria under actual security charters.
- For funding, maintain separate beneficial ledgers: senior Great de Villiers; four French Majors; Belgian de Bréval; Swiss de Corvelles; Lessers; corporate co-venturers; independently managed capital. No automatic adding of national infrastructure assets to personal fortune.
