# House de Villiers — Final Great-House Classification and Canon Reconciliation

**Status: USER-LOCKED HOUSE IDENTITY — 11 October 2026**  
**Source:** User's explicit clarification of the series of worldbuilding decisions. This ruling supersedes all earlier drafts describing de Villiers as an active cadet or de Varenne as an extant Great House.

## Current in-world canon

- **House de Villiers is a recognized Great House**, a sovereign peer in the international hidden Order.
- **House de Varenne does not exist as a separate House in the settled fictional setting.** Do not create a de Varenne head, court, estate, cadet link, coat of arms, rival line or member from old drafts.
- **de Villiers was not promoted, restored, merged, or placed in succession to de Varenne inside the story.** Any older narrative that makes de Villiers subordinate to de Varenne is superseded authoring material, not in-world history.
- **Henri Alexandre de Villiers** is the established spouse of **Catherine Anne Ravenscroft de Villiers**, and their earlier-approved immediate family/biographical characteristics remain valid to the extent they do not depend on the discarded de Varenne status. This decision alone does not assign Henri the constitutional Great-House Headship, international Council mandate, new title, residence, or revised wealth.
- **Henri's earlier Master A dossier image was uploaded on 11 October 2026.** It confirms his approved portrait, family, professional offices, apparent age and personal/household finances. Its old subordinate-House ancestry and former de Varenne liaison text are obsolete and must not be copied into revised records; his **facial identity remains approved**.
- Other attached family members (including **Élodie Catherine de Villiers**, **Laurent Henri de Villiers**, and **Sophie Eleanor de Villiers**) retain their established genealogies and marriages. Their rank and dossiers should be validated against current sources before any new Great-House-specific duties are created.

## Development-history note — out of universe, not a fictional event

1. Original French Great House concept was named **de Varenne**.
2. **de Villiers** was created in connection with Henri's Ravenscroft marriage, initially mistaken for the Great House when drafting Henri's dossier.
3. As an intermediate continuity patch, de Villiers was temporarily treated as subordinate.
4. The final authoring decision **eliminated the de Varenne concept and substituted de Villiers as the sole Great House** across the universe.

This sequence is solely the history of the worldbuilding project, **not historical events that characters lived through**. Never backfill a de Varenne-to-de Villiers political transition into the Order's chronicle.
