## Canonical facts protected

House de Villiers, *The Architects of Continuity*, is an autonomous French Great House. **de Varenne is not a House in the completed fictional setting**; do not invent an in-world de Varenne transition. The approved arms are a burgundy shield with ivory fortified gatehouse and antique-gold surveyor's dividers; the rejected blue bridge/waves/star scheme belongs neither to this House nor its approved seal. No motto is approved.

The constitutional seat is Château de Montrevaux (fictional Côte-d'Or, Burgundy); Paris Hôtel de Villiers is the institutional office. Moulin des Ormes is Henri and Catherine's private retreat. Modern Heads are Étienne Louis (1956–1988), Charles Étienne (1988–2018), and Philippe Étienne (2018 onward); Adrien Philippe is designated successor. Henri Alexandre is Charles's younger brother and Philippe's uncle, not a concurrent Head. Élodie, Laurent and Sophie are Henri and Catherine's children and Philippe's first cousins.

The subordinate network comprises **six directly subordinate Major Houses**, of which two are blood cadets (de Villiers de la Loire, de Villiers de Provence) and four are nonblood bound Majors (d'Aubrecourt, Montferrand, de Bréval, de Corvelles). Eight international Lesser Houses are approved: de Lamberval, Vandenmeer, de Bauffre, d'Orvalet under de Bréval; Rochat-Valerne, de Cressens, Perrin-Duval under de Corvelles; Bellafonte under de Villiers de Provence. Proposed French Lesser Houses are not ratified. The seven-seat Conseil de Maison is separate from the international Council of Nine; the latter's mandate is not assigned by Great-House status alone.
