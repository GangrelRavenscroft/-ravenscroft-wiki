## Established present identity

House de Villiers is the recognized French Great House known as **The Architects of Continuity**. Its ancestral constitutional seat is **Château de Montrevaux** in Burgundy, with **Hôtel de Villiers** in Paris as its institutional headquarters. Philippe Étienne de Villiers is Head since 2018. Its dominant specialties are infrastructure engineering, durable built systems, preservation, energy and water facilities and patient investment. Villiers Continental Works and Henri Alexandre de Villiers's Chairman Emeritus career are previously established. ## 2026 strategic position

De Villiers enjoys deep French roots and institutional links in Belgium and French-speaking Switzerland; it exercises authority over affiliated House networks, not public governments. Its marriage alliances with Ravenscroft and Amaru are established. Wycliffe, Falkenrath and Veyrholm offer natural spheres of cooperation and professional competition, not presumed historic treaties. Preservation versus technical adaptation and Parisian direction versus independent affiliates create credible political tensions.
