These ranges are a **proposed financial scale**, not the approved 2026 valuation of the House.

## 2. Four institutional pillars and two oversight functions — proposed

### A. Compagnie Patrimoniale de Villiers (CPV) — senior patrimony and capital office

**Status:** newly proposed **common principal-House** capital/stewardship vehicle, legally separate from Henri personally, from cadets and from public clients.

- **Working headquarters:** Hôtel de Villiers, Paris; professional investment and administration offices, with separate legal entities wherever needed.
- **Function:** voting control of specifically identified portfolio subsidiaries, minority co-investments, private property portfolios, infrastructure positions, liquidity reserves and financing / treasury.
- **Governance:** appointed professional chief executive and independent risk/investment committees. Philippe holds House constitutional direction, not an unqualified right to trade client accounts. Council's unfilled Steward of Capital seat will have audit/mandate oversight, not absolute ownership.
- **Limits:** an investment fund managing $1T in client assets does not mean the House owns $1T; only its net legal equity or contractual economic interest belongs to the family.

### B. Villiers Continental Works (VCW) — established enterprise, modern operating outline proposed

**LOCKED EXISTING ENTITY:** Villiers Continental Works and Henri's Chairman Emeritus career role. Everything numerical or corporate in this subsection is **proposed**.

**2026 operating envelope OPTION:** revenue **€22–35 billion**, workforce **45,000–75,000**, and enterprise valuation roughly **€35–60 billion**, subject to approval and later ownership/financial reconciliation. **These are all separate units**: euro-denominated gross turnover, persons employed, and total enterprise value including finance/debt. No figure is family net worth by default.

- Business lanes: technically demanding transportation civil works, complex asset maintenance, restoration of major masonry/steel structures, energy-grid installations and water treatment facilities, project management and engineering diagnostics.
- Requires independent boards, site-level safety management, statutory compliance, procurement audit, professional engineers, credible contracting limits and the ability to refuse technically unsound House requests.
- Henri's Emeritus role provides respect and specialist advice, not a duplicate executive chain of command with the serving board.
- **Company scale limit:** the proposed €35–60B VCW enterprise value is only one component of the House's broader economic system; it cannot magically account for the proposed ~$955B principal fortune.
- **Proposed decision to develop later:** what percentage of VCW shares (and effective control) CPV/senior House, Henri or independently owned investors actually hold. Avoid implying Henri's personal $500M means he owns the whole group.

### C. Institut de la Continuité des Ouvrages — applied science, technical education and failure analysis

**Status:** proposed House-sponsored institution with professional educational governance, neither another megacorporation nor an Order indoctrination factory.

- Research and training: asset-life prediction, structural health monitoring, corrosion, concrete longevity, civil reliability, hydrology, dam/bridge risk, aging transport infrastructure and engineering incident analysis.
- Apprenticeship, university and professional fellowship programs are available to qualified non-Order citizens. A patroned student is not automatically inducted into the Order.
- Potential joint seminars with Cadet Loire (hydrology), Cadet Provence (coastal / thermal exposure), d'Aubrecourt (rail), Montferrand (geotechnics) and the direct Lesser Giraud-Lenoir (control-system security), if branches are ratified.
- Governing board and actual university partners to be documented case by case. Shared technical expertise does not confer constitutional control over the member organizations.

### D. Fondation de Villiers pour le Patrimoine et les Sciences Appliquées — philanthropic trust

**Status:** proposed independent charitable foundation with accountable professional trustees.

- Restoration of historic works, grants to academic engineers, architectural conservation, public education, environmental restoration, technical apprenticeships.
- Public-benefit restrictions and trustee governance prevent House discretionary diversion of dedicated charitable assets.
- Possible diplomacy and goodwill as secondary outcomes, not a fictional automatic lever to commandeer French universities, regulators or UNESCO.

### E. Archive de Montrevaux / Office de la Continuité — protected constitutional staff

**Status:** proposed internal office anchored at Château de Montrevaux and a separate undisclosed continuity backup.

- Custody of verified senior genealogy, headship authentication, dynastic charters, cadet property maps, legal records, crisis succession and continuity frameworks.
- Warden of Dynastic Continuity seat provides independent authentication / archival oversight with defined protected records, not spending control.
- A protected backup does not become an alternate constitutional House seat.
- Information compartmentalization remains real; cousins, married-in partners and younger professionals do not automatically see historic Order intelligence.

### F. Bureau des Accords et des Risques — professional external / project coordination

**Status:** proposed small Paris-based legal-commercial and treaty/contract review function serving senior House. It is not a French government ministry.

- Civil contracts, public procurement safeguards, bid disputes, environmental obligations, joint ventures, emergency continuity obligations, Great-House liaison coordination.
- Senior Henri may consult on exceptional strategic works; his Tier II Order standing does not entitle him to bypass contractual approval and lawful House office procedures.
- Other Houses and public customers may disagree, refuse projects or seek independent arbitration.

## 3. Example investment mechanisms, limits and dependencies

**Direct ownership:** a House-owned engineering company or a family-beneficial majority equity position has enforceable governance rights, subject to regulation and the board's real duties.

**Minority influence:** CPV may own 15% of a utility investment company with board arrangements and long contracts; that grants documented governance influence, **not** beneficial ownership of the whole utility system or its government concessions.

**Contracting:** VCW can win tenders and serve independent clients. Revenue from government work does not confer political sovereignty or ownership of the asset it repairs.

**Alliance / co-investment:** de Villiers may cooperate with other Great Houses while keeping each party's capital, security rules and governance distinct. A Ravenscroft-linked project does not make Ravenscroft owner of all de Villiers affiliates, or the reverse.

**Patronage:** engineers, scholarship recipients, local firms and professionals can be bound by honorable reciprocal obligations, but technical competence and labor markets give them independent incentives and mobility.

**Covert protection:** some House projects warrant discreet protective operations and classified continuity plans, but these are bounded and politically expensive. They do not substitute for legitimate engineering or allow blanket capture of government inspections.
