## Approved constitutional structure

**Sovereign Head:** Philippe Étienne de Villiers (born 17 April 1964), head of recognized Great House de Villiers since 2018. **Constitutional seat:** Château de Montrevaux, fictional Burgundy. **Institutional center:** Hôtel de Villiers, Paris.

All **six Major Houses report DIRECTLY to sovereign Great House de Villiers**, never to another Major. Cadet means a separate **blood-related** House at Major rank; the other four are **bound, non-blood Majors** with separate histories, legitimate property and succession. Their chartered regions are spheres *inside the hidden House network*, not public government, modern territorial sovereignty or beneficial ownership of all local companies.

The Swiss House has **no general jurisdiction in Zürich, Basel or German/Italian-speaking Switzerland** merely by virtue of its Swiss name. It may hold specific investments and partners outside Romandy, with appropriate reciprocal jurisdiction procedures. Geneva is a **multi-House international center**: the de Corvelles charter governs only de Villiers-affiliated families, institutions and House obligations there, not the entire city. Similarly, de Bréval's Belgium-wide liaison role must not be mistaken for ownership of Belgium's government, Brussels's international bodies, all Flemish commerce or Belgian public infrastructure.

### Seven-seat Conseil de Maison — locked structural separation

The **existing locked seven seats are NOT expanded** just because there are six Major Houses. The two **reserved blood-cadet Major seats** remain separately designated. The four **non-blood bound Major Heads do NOT receive automatic voting seats**; fair consultation, notice, appeal and portfolio-specific participation need their own charter. No named blood-cadet Head has yet been ratified to occupy either Council seat.

Steward of Capital and Common Estates (not selected); 7. Warden of Dynastic Continuity (not selected). Charles retains a nonvoting elder role. ### Nation-specific network rules

**Belgium — de Bréval:** Has the principal authority for formally affiliated de Villiers network Houses/families, protective liaison and contract coordination in Belgium. Its center of gravity should be Wallonia plus discrete Brussels institutional relationships; industrial and maritime links in Flanders can be negotiated with independent local actors and other international peer networks, never assumed sovereign by default.

**French-speaking Switzerland — de Corvelles:** Has principal network authority in Suisse romande; must distinguish independent Swiss government/cantonal jurisdiction, banking/legal entities and other Great-House operations. Local issues in Geneva can be supervised by specifically chartered Lessers (if approved), and inter-House conflict must be handled by protocol and neutral arbitration, not blanket command.

**Luxembourg:** **Not assigned to a seventh Major.** It is a proposed lighter cross-border financial/technical liaison, potentially chartered under de Bréval, under the sovereign House directly, or held through an independent allied family. **Which is chosen is open**.

**Monaco / Riviera:** A proposed portfolio relationship for Provence Major, serviced by a suitable local Lesser House or envoy **only if separately ratified**. Monaco's sovereignty remains separate.

**North African / other Francophone project sites:** primarily contractual activity, alumni/professional patronage or independent partner Houses; not additional constitutional overseas territory by default. Any later durable local House would need its own reviewed charter.
