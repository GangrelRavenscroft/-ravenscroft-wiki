# House Marenesi — Regional Major House, 2026

**STATUS: APPROVED BACKGROUND CANON — October 11, 2026.** Existing canon remains binding: **House Marenesi** is an independently descended (non-cadet) Major House, directly subordinate to Great House Valdieri; current Head **Giorgio Pietro Marenesi**, born **1970**, works in maritime insurance and commercial risk; its separate 2026 net worth is **US$350–650 million**. Its primary regional House mandate is **eastern Liguria and the La Spezia-facing commercial corridor**. It maintains a longstanding commercial presence in Genoa without becoming subordinate to **Claudia's separate Major-ranked cadet House Valdieri di Genova**. The immediate household, birth years, careers, seat, enterprise names and broad post-restoration allegiance model below were approved. Exact birth dates, detailed beneficial-ownership allocations, heraldry and a precise binding instrument/date remain open. This is a Major-House background profile; full Ravenscroft-style personal dossiers are not needed.

## 1. A Ligurian merchant dynasty with its own sovereignty

Marenesi became prosperous by remaining reliable in uncertain maritime markets. It understands marine underwriting, commercial liability, merchant-property structures, the financial needs of maritime enterprises, specialized business records and the risks faced by medium-sized shipping and export firms. It can act as a regional political and institutional liaison inside the Valdieri network while preserving the professional reputation that makes it valuable outside the Order.

**Established dynastic seat:** **Palazzo delle Torri**, a fictional historic merchant palazzo in Genoa, preserved as an active residence and family office. The property is distinct from the Genova cadet's established **Palazzo di Levante**. **Eastern Ligurian operating office:** a comparatively modest commercial building in the La Spezia area, emphasizing that Marenesi's **regional responsibility need not coincide with the location of its ancestral home or all business interests**.

## 4. Approved historical allegiance resolution

A critical continuity issue is that Valdieri was demoted to **Major House** after World War II. **A Major House cannot constitutionally be subordinate to another Major House** under the recently locked hierarchy.

- **Before 1946:** Marenesi exists as an independent merchant Major House. It may have longstanding contacts with Valdieri and Genova, but has no need to be a hidden legal subordinate of a rival Major House.
- **1946–2023:** As Valdieri rebuilds while Major-ranked, Marenesi remains an **independent allied Major House**, cooperating commercially with the diminished senior line and separately with the Genova cadet. Neither relationship automatically transfers sovereignty.
- **2023 Great-House restoration:** The re-elevated Valdieri becomes a constitutional authority legally capable of receiving binding Major-House allegiance. Its prior alliances provide the groundwork, but no retroactive subordinate status should be invented.
- **After 2023; late 2024 or 2025 remains a working date range:** Marenesi negotiates a directly bound regional Major-House charter with **Lorenzo**, retaining its own leadership and patrimony. The precise year should be approved later. **Claudia is a peer Major Head, never Marenesi's sovereign superior**.
- **2026:** Giorgio's eastern Ligurian mandate reflects an arrangement designed to coexist with Genova's Genoa/western Ligurian primary sphere, without forcibly transferring every preexisting Genoese property or customer.

This distinction means Marenesi joins Valdieri from a position of real capability. It receives institutional protection, access to technical and capital networks, diplomatic relevance inside the Order and a stable Great-House sponsor. Valdieri receives credible regional representation, expert risk judgment and connections into maritime industries.

## 5. Proposed regional charter

- **Direct reporting:** Giorgio communicates formally with the principal House and its delegated regional-liaison office on protected issues. He is not required to obtain Claudia's permission for an eastern Ligurian regional matter or an ordinary Genoese commercial contract.
- **Regional mandate:** connections with Eastern Ligurian House-aligned firms, protected professional networks, heritage-commercial property and regional maritime insurance systems.
- **Own affairs:** maintains Marenesi succession, corporate ownership, finances, staff, family decisions, local patronage and professional investments.
- **Overlap with Genova:** joint ventures and voluntary business agreements can exist, but the Genova Major House may not assert dynastic jurisdiction over Marenesi or its employees. Disputes about actual commercial contracts follow their governing law; disputes about two peer Major Houses' charters rise to Lorenzo.
- **No criminal-network shortcut:** marine insurance, property and port businesses do not imply organized-crime ownership or participation merely due to Ligurian geography. Any clandestine relationships require separate canon.
- **Compact boundaries:** no public governmental institution or independent port authority becomes Marenesi-owned simply because it lies in the House's regional territory.

## 6. Personality and political relationships

**Giorgio and Claudia** should be able to negotiate agreements while privately assessing each other's ambitions. Giorgio needs the confidence to tell Claudia “no” as a peer without treating every disagreement as rebellion. Claudia has no reason to approve another Major's intrusion into her actual chartered regional obligations, but she may profit from shared merchant relationships.

**Giorgio and Lorenzo** have a relationship shaped by the restoration. Lorenzo values the proof that an independent established Major voluntarily binds itself to Valdieri; Giorgio values Great-House support but will defend his own family charter.

**Giorgio and Beatrice Roccabruna** share a practical interest in contract reliability despite having different professional backgrounds. Their relationship can become a productive counterweight to the more emotionally charged Genova/Marenesi rivalry.
