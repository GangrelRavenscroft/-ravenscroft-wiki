House Ravenscroft

The dominant North American Great House: an old dynastic institution whose modern power is expressed through family governance, patronage, education, intelligence, security, institutional influence, and long-term stewardship.

Principal Seat

Secret Hudson Valley estate. A Virginia property serves as a decoy/public-facing seat within the wider House system.

Current Headship

House headship remains distinct from Order leadership. The succession line runs Edmund → Alexander → Lucian → Sebastian, with Lucian holding current House headship in 2026.

Family

Open the Ravenscroft genealogy to move through migrated branches and individual dossiers.

Order Relationship

Ravenscroft is deeply embedded in the international Order but does not own it. Blood status and House office do not automatically determine Order rank or clearance.

Formation

House education emphasizes history, strategy, rhetoric, negotiation, motive-reading, discretion, counter-manipulation, institutional fluency, and a broad Ravenscroft repertoire.

Residence Rule

Most blood family members maintain primary residence in the United States. International residences are important but intentionally not the default primary base.
