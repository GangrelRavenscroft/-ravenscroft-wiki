House Record

House Ravenscroft

The most influential Great House in the modern hidden order: a comparatively young constitutional peer whose power grew from North American independence, institutional adaptability, and the postwar rise of the United States.

Principal Seat

Secret Hudson Valley estate. A Virginia property serves as a decoy/public-facing seat within the wider House system.

Current Headship

House headship remains distinct from Order leadership. The succession line runs Edmund → Alexander → Lucian → Sebastian, with Lucian holding current House headship in 2026.

Family

Open the Ravenscroft genealogy to move through migrated branches and individual dossiers.

Order Relationship

Ravenscroft is deeply embedded in the international Order but does not own it. Blood status, House office, Order rank, clearance, and constitutional representation remain distinct.

Formation

House education prepares members for the Order, but formal induction teaches and binds them to the Compact and the Histories shared by all inducted members.

Residence Rule

Most blood family members maintain primary residence in the United States. International residences are important but intentionally not the default primary base.

01

From Cadet House to Great House

The lineage that became Ravenscroft originated as an American cadet branch of House Falkenrath. During the American War of Independence the branch broke from Falkenrath authority and established itself as an independent House.

Independence did not confer Great-House standing. Ravenscroft spent the following decades proving durable sovereignty, succession, institutional depth, security capacity, patronage, and North American consequence. Its eventual early-nineteenth-century recognition became an important precedent for the principle that an established Great House cannot permanently deny the independent peer status of a former cadet line that has demonstrably outgrown dependency.

02

Modern Primacy

Ravenscroft's current primacy is informal rather than constitutional. It has one Conclave Voice like every other Great House and remains subject to the Compact.

The House's rise to first-among-peers influence occurred during the post-World War II transformation of American power. Alexander Ravenscroft was instrumental in converting expanding U.S. industrial, financial, scientific, technological, intelligence, educational, diplomatic, and logistical reach into durable House networks and international relevance.

Ravenscroft is not necessarily strongest in every individual domain. Its advantage is breadth, adaptability, institutional reach, alliance depth, and the ability to coordinate many forms of power at once. That position can be lost through internal fracture, strategic overreach, alliance collapse, institutional decline, failure to adapt, or the rise of a more consequential peer.

03

Constitutional Representation

As Head of House Ravenscroft, Lucian holds the House Voice in the Conclave and may exercise it personally or accredit a Conclave Delegate. The current standing delegate is intentionally not yet assigned in the archive.

Ravenscroft's Councilor and Deputy Councilor will likewise be assigned after the relevant current family members are developed. These offices are separate from House Headship and from the Grandmaster's authority within the international Order.

Individual officeholders will be filled from established characters as their dossiers and pages are completed rather than invented solely to populate the organizational chart.
