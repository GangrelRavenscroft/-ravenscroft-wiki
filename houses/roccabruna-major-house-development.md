# House Roccabruna — Regional Major House, 2026

**STATUS: APPROVED BACKGROUND CANON — October 11, 2026.** Existing canon remains fixed: *Casa Roccabruna* is a non-blood Major House directly bound to sovereign Great House Valdieri; current Head **Dr. Beatrice Ludovica Roccabruna**, born **1969**, is a civil engineer specializing in hydrogeology; independent net worth **US$500–800 million**; principal regional authority is **southwest Piedmont and the Cuneo–Turin corridor**. The established constitutional seat **Rocca delle Fonti** remains directly under the principal Valdieri House despite proximity to Roccabruna's mandate. The named immediate family, birth years, roles, Villa Roccabruna, enterprise names and broad household/history concepts below were subsequently approved. Exact birthdays, asset-by-asset valuations, heraldry and the formal post-2023 binding date remain open. This is a background Major-House profile; exhaustive Ravenscroft-style dossiers are not required.

## 1. A working dynasty rather than a ceremonial family

Roccabruna's prestige comes from decades of reliable civil engineering, environmental risk assessment, mountain infrastructure and industrial-property stewardship. It is wealthy on its own account, owns firms and property, and is a genuine regional authority for House Valdieri's network, not a collection of employees. Its loyalty is secured by meaningful technical contracts, protected institutional status, independent ownership and a tradition of fulfilling difficult obligations. Roccabruna's House Head answers **directly to Lorenzo**, never through Genova or Canavese. It may work commercially with all of them.

**Established family seat:** **Villa Roccabruna**, a fictional late-eighteenth/early-nineteenth-century working estate outside Cuneo with a historical garden, service buildings, family library and offices. **Professional base:** an independently leased or owned engineering headquarters in Turin with regional offices near Cuneo. Neither location is Rocca delle Fonti, which belongs to the sovereign Great House.

## 4. Approved broad political history

- **1910s–1940s:** Roccabruna exists as an independent technical and landed lineage with profitable industrial and civil-works relationships. It is not a blood branch of Valdieri and need not support either faction in the 1934–1946 civil war. Its engineers may have maintained assets for various clients amid the disruption; exact role is open.
- **1946–1980s:** A sustained professional alliance develops with the diminished House Valdieri, which is then a **Major House**, not yet sovereign Great. Roccabruna remains a distinct Major House and **does not formally answer to another Major House**.
- **1990s–2023:** Valdieri's restoration advances; Roccabruna provides valuable operating and infrastructure competence through contracts and reciprocal alliances rather than a fictional retroactive period of constitutional subordination.
- **After Valdieri's 2023 re-elevation:** **2024 is a working possible year** for the direct-to-Great-House Major charter, with Roccabruna retaining its own assets and regional Headship. The binding instrument and exact effective date remain open. The new allegiance should be recorded as voluntary negotiated status with protection and real benefits, not an unmentioned compulsory takeover.
- **2026:** Beatrice occupies a formal regional authority position for southwestern Piedmont while remaining a serious technical professional and independent owner.

## 5. Regional powers and boundaries

Roccabruna coordinates recognized House relationships, protected technical interests and appropriate local diplomacy across its southwest Piedmont mandate. Its technical contacts may include real municipalities, insurers, manufacturers, universities and public utilities, **none automatically House-owned**. It does not own civil government and cannot order independent municipal officials around merely through rank.

**Direct sovereign exception:** Rocca delle Fonti, its archives and its constitutionally protected personnel answer to Lorenzo and the Warden of Constitutional Continuity, not Roccabruna. **Direct Lesser exception:** Serravento's direct allegiance also remains with the Great House, even when its alpine activities overlap Roccabruna's geography. Coordination is a duty; hierarchical absorption is forbidden without charter revision.

## 6. Relationship with Lorenzo, Matteo and other Majors

Lorenzo respects Beatrice's reliability and appreciates Roccabruna's independent competence, but may find her resistance to ceremonial prestige inconvenient. Matteo often agrees with her technical approach; that does not make him Roccabruna's superior or give him authority over its property before he becomes Head. Claudia and Tommaso can engage Roccabruna as peer Major Heads; family blood status gives them dynastic privileges but not professional superiority in hydrogeology.
