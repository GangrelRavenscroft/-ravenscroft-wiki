# House Valdieri — Regional Charter Operations, Appeals and Lesser-House Profiles (2026)

**STATUS: DEVELOPMENT PROPOSAL — NOT LOCKED.** Complements the **locked** broad regional mandates in [Valdieri regional governance](valdieri-regional-governance-development.md), [the established four-Major / five-Lesser hierarchy](valdieri-subordinate-houses-2026-proposal.md) and the existing seven-seat **Consiglio di Casa Valdieri**. This proposal does not change rank, sovereignty, pre-existing financial ownership, council voting thresholds, historical allegiance or approved regional assignments.

## 1. Suggested operating rule: direct Great-House authority, limited delegation

A regional **Major House Head** is Valdieri's appointed/chartered regional **House-network authority**, not a provincial governor. Every Major maintains its separate Headship, private property, professional staff, businesses, succession and patronage consistent with its charter. The Great House may direct performance of lawful **House obligations**, not seize assets or commandeer subsidiaries without separate legal authority.

- **Quarterly confidential regional memorandum (proposed):** Each Major Head reports directly to Lorenzo or a formally designated principal-House receiving office about protected House-network relationships, significant charter disputes, developing threats to institutions and material conflicts requiring Great-House coordination. This is **not a demand for consolidated corporate accounting** of privately owned subordinate firms.
- **Annual House review (proposed):** Principal House and regional Majors review the next year's responsibilities, extraordinary projects and cooperation protocols at Rocca delle Fonti or a secure Milan site. Attendance does not create regional Majors' voting rights on the seven-seat House Council.
- **Immediate event notification (proposed):** A Major alerts Lorenzo's authorized office promptly if a succession emergency, Compact breach, protected House asset incident or credible peer-House constitutional confrontation arises. Security and emergency coordination use the proper chartered personnel, not an ad hoc power grab.
- **Steward of Assets:** Advice and checks are limited to principal-House capital risk, commitments and separately agreed joint projects. Steward cannot consolidate Roccabruna, Marenesi, Genova, Canavese or any Lesser balance sheet merely because of charter allegiance.
- **Warden of Constitutional Continuity:** Maintains the authoritative *central instruments* of subordination, succession acknowledgments, direct exceptions and protected records. No automatic general power to inspect all unrelated internal family records or change a private heir.

**Suggested mandate categories:** Each charter identifies (a) functional region, (b) specific House relationships managed, (c) existing direct Great-House exceptions, (d) which Lesser House if any answers to that Major, (e) protocol for emergencies, and (f) appeal routes. It need not draw a fictional hard border around all commercial activity in an Italian province.

| **Canavese / Tommaso** | Canavese/Ivrea and northern-Piedmont industrial and land relationships | Valcerra serves clients across Italy, without extending Canavese's constitutional regional jurisdiction |
| **Roccabruna / Beatrice** | Cuneo and the southwest Piedmont/Turin–Cuneo professional network | **Rocca delle Fonti** and directly attached **Serravento** are protected direct-Great-House functions, even when nearby |

**Senior House reserved nodes:** Rocca delle Fonti and protected archives, Palazzo Valdieri/Milan principal administration, the constitutional acts of the Great-House Head and central House bodies, plus any subsequent specifically recorded sovereign instrument. This is an **exception system inside the House network**, not a map of civil governmental control.

**Disputed jurisdiction:** Determine primarily by **which chartered House institution/relationship** is at issue, not simply the postal address of the commercial project. For multi-regional ventures, the interested Major Heads may appoint a lead coordinator for the project; this does **not** transfer peer House rank or ownership.

## 3. Proposed peer-House dispute and appeal procedure

1. **Commercial dispute:** Begin with the relevant companies' normal contracts, independent boards, legal counsel and applicable civil law. A commercial disagreement between the Genova and Marenesi groups is not automatically a dynastic case.
2. **Charter or authority disagreement between peer Majors:** The two Heads confer privately; either may request a procedural record from the principal House's **Warden of Constitutional Continuity**. If unresolved, it is referred **directly to Lorenzo** and lawful central House authority. No Major hears appeals from its peer Major as sovereign judge.
3. **Lesser House vs. its chartered superior:** Begin with the immediate superior (Lorenzo if a directly bound Lesser, Claudia for Portelivo, Tommaso for Valcerra/Boscariva). Constitutional grievances involving prohibited confiscation, extinction of House status or unlawful charter change may be appealed to Lorenzo and the Warden's procedural custody.
4. **Protected structural acts:** Where an act falls into the already established protected class, require **five of the seven Consiglio seats AND the lawful Head's concurrence**; no ordinary officer or appointed coordinator can waive that rule. Do not presume every day-to-day commercial contract is protected.
5. **Conflict involving Lorenzo himself:** The Warden records the procedural objection and relevant evidence for authorized review under the House's existing lawful charter; the Warden does not replace Lorenzo, elect a new Head or invent a superior private tribunal. The exceptional constitutional review forum remains to be specified before canonization.
