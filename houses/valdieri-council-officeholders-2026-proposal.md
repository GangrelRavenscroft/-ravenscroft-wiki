# House Valdieri — 2026 Council Officeholders — Locked Canon

**STATUS: APPROVED AND LOCKED CANON — October 11, 2026.** The two offices and their seats on the **seven-member Consiglio di Casa Valdieri** were already canon; the user has now also approved the incumbents, births, education, broad careers, personalities and personal relationships recorded here. **Still open:** precise appointment instruments and exact effective dates after the 2023 restoration; specifics of Order clearance and operational authorization. This is a background leadership record, not a new Council tier.

## Existing council: fixed

The established seven voting seats are: (1) **Lorenzo Vittorio Valdieri**, Head; (2) **Matteo Lorenzo Valdieri**, heir; (3) **Dr. Elena Beatrice Montanari Valdieri**, appointed senior consort; (4) **Claudia Renata Valdieri di Genova**, cadet Head; (5) **Tommaso Enrico Valdieri di Canavese**, cadet Head; (6) **Steward of Assets**, incumbent identified below; (7) **Warden of Constitutional Continuity**, incumbent identified below. Routine council recommendations use a simple majority; **protected structural acts require five of seven plus the lawful Head's concurrence**. This is not the Order's nine-seat International Council. Neither independent Major House Roccabruna nor Marenesi receives a separate council seat by virtue of its regional House rank.

## Steward of Assets / Sovrintendente al Patrimonio

**Full name:** **Dr. Riccardo Leone Ferrante**  
**Birth:** **February 16, 1970** (56 in October 2026)  
**Status:** Italian; **non-Valdieri blood**, appointed principal-House officer; not the Head of a bound Major or Lesser House. He has a separate private life and modest personal wealth compared with the dynasty.  
**Education:** Economics and finance at **Bocconi University**, Milan (degree completed 1993); further professional work in corporate restructuring, risk governance and institutional capital allocation.  
**Career:** Begins with Italian institutional banking and corporate-risk roles in the 1990s, then works in private capital stewardship. Recruited to **Compagnia Patrimoniale Valdieri** in **2008**, with responsibility for consolidated ownership records and major commitments, and formally sworn into the reinstated Great-House stewardship office **after the 2023 restoration** (precise date open). He is comfortable with the legal and operational gap between private beneficial ownership, common House patrimony, foundation property and merely influenced companies.

**Role and limits:** Supervises the principal House treasury framework, reserve and liquidity planning, succession-safe ownership documentation, capital commitments and stress-testing; advises legitimate independent corporate boards rather than owning their decisions. **He cannot absorb Genova, Canavese, Roccabruna, Marenesi or Lesser-House independent balance sheets**. He cannot authorize protected charter changes without the established council mechanism.

**Temperament:** Exacting, practical, deliberate rather than theatrical. He is willing to tell Lorenzo when symbolic Great-House expenditures threaten liquidity; views financial credibility as the precondition for reversing Valdieri's weakened postwar status. With **Matteo**, he respects engineering expertise but insists that industrial investment clear measurable hurdles. With **Claudia**, trust is possible only if he consistently respects Genova's separate patrimony.

**Personal texture:** Milan is his professional base, but he regularly attends House sessions at Rocca delle Fonti. Prefers ordinary professional discretion, long-distance hiking and restoration of small mechanical instruments over gala visibility. Publicly presents as a private-capital executive, not an aristocratic enforcer.

**Apparent age and future visuals:** Non-Great-House blood; privileged medical access might modestly preserve his appearance, but do not depict an implausibly young man in his thirties. Apparent-age visual guidance: **early fifties**.

## Warden of Constitutional Continuity / Custode della Continuità

**Full name:** **Avv. Dr. Teresa Maddalena Bellandi**  
**Birth:** **November 4, 1963** (62 in October 2026)  
**Status:** Italian; **non-Valdieri blood**, independent legal-historical and archival specialist; sworn guardian of the constitutional record. The title derives from office, not hereditary House Headship.  
**Education:** Law at the **University of Turin** (laurea completed 1987); research in legal history, dynastic instruments, historical charters and archival evidentiary practice. Maintains professional credentials appropriate to her actual legal work; this is **not** a claim of power over civil courts.
**Career:** Specialist work in private archives, succession documentation and heritage-institution governance throughout the 1990s. Began assisting House Valdieri's constitutional and archival apparatus in **2004**; later undertook the painstaking reconciliation of wartime, demotion-era and recovery documents and was designated Warden upon or soon after **2023 restoration** (exact appointment date open).

**Role and limits:** Authenticates legitimate succession, regency, protected House instruments, cadet charters, archival custody and proper recording of the 2023 restoration. She advises on instrument validity and may issue formal procedural objections, but **cannot select a successor**, override lawful voting results, extinguish a cadet's property rights or claim ownership over the records of every subordinate House. The surviving Genova wardship and restitution records are preserved, contextualized and access-controlled—not rewritten into a convenient fairy tale. Her mandate remains distinct from civil state jurisdiction and the international Council.

**Temperament:** Calmly formidable, rigorous with evidence, exceptionally resistant to retroactive reinterpretations that flatter powerful relatives. Lorenzo values her credibility even when she makes restoration-era politics uncomfortable. She is frequently an intermediary when Claudia's historical grievances and the senior line's legitimacy intersect. With Riccardo she cooperates on ownership records but can refuse any proposed change to constitutional classification or archive authenticity made for balance-sheet convenience.

**Personal texture:** Keeps a separate primary home in Turin and maintains professional ties with historical researchers and conservators. Appreciates chamber music, rare-document conservation and northern Italian regional history. Publicly recognized as an archival/legal-history specialist; inner constitutional duties are known only to appropriately cleared members.

**Apparent age and future visuals:** Non-Great-House blood; apparent-age visual guidance **late fifties to early sixties**. Her authority should look lived-in and scholarly, not dependent on glamorous styling.

## Why these appointments strengthen Valdieri

The restored House is powerful but much poorer and politically more vulnerable than peer Great Houses. Appointing two competent **non-blood professionals**, instead of adding unknown Valdieri cousins, makes the small council more credible: Riccardo demonstrates disciplined capital stewardship while Teresa ensures that the postwar constitutional record cannot be quietly rewritten. Neither is automatically a member of the Order's international Council. Any personal Order induction, clearance or hidden operational authorization must be documented independently rather than inferred from House employment.

This produces a useful political dynamic without changing the fixed **five-of-seven plus Head concurrence** threshold: the existing four blood-line seats (Lorenzo, Matteo, Claudia and Tommaso), Elena's consort seat and these two independent officeholders must cooperate for protected structural actions. The offices have no general voting authority over independent Major- and Lesser-House commercial decisions.
