## Constitutional and visual continuity maintained

- House Valdieri regained **Great-House status in 2023** after its 1946–2023 Major period. The *international* Council seat lost to Ravenscroft was not restored. Its own **seven-seat Consiglio di Casa** is separate from the Order's nine-seat Council.
- The two Valdieri-blood Major cadets **Genova** and **Canavese**, plus independently descended nonblood Majors **Roccabruna** and **Marenesi**, each answer directly to Great House Valdieri, not to one another. Five independently owned Lesser Houses follow their previously ratified Great House / Genova / Canavese reporting paths.
- The approved armorial identity remains **silver three-arch bridge over waves and a gold star on blue**. Generated decorative lions, alternative shields or suggested mottos do **not** alter it.
- Principal Great-House wealth **US$3.5–5B** excludes separate cadet, bound Major, Lesser and third-party patrimonies. Protected structural acts still require **five of the seven House councilors plus concurrence of the lawful Head**.
- **Giulia:** Her individual 2026 facial canon remains her approved Batch 2 Valdieri portrait. The Batch 9 Giulia–Elias couple picture is a separately approved **relationship illustration**, not her still-pending Ravenscroft-styled primary Master B dossier portrait.
- **Marenesi maritime scene:** Historic ship imagery is only incidental/cinematic styling; it is not a claim that full-size Age-of-Sail fleets operate in a real contemporary 2026 harbor. If a future story illustration needs modern vessels, use a separately reviewed corrected derivative, not retroactive historical canon.
