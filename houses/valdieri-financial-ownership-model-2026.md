## A credible economic weakness

The principal House is **asset-rich relative to ordinary families and liquidity-constrained relative to recognized Great Houses**. Large stakes are in productive but illiquid firms, estate maintenance and long-term institutional investments. A smaller amount of House-common capital is immediately deployable without compromising independent security, archives, education and continuity.

The 2023 Great-House restoration demonstrates constitutional sovereignty, credible reserves and institutional depth, **not comparable wealth to Wycliffe or Ravenscroft**. Genova and Canavese add political and industrial resilience through their own resources, but neither transfers title of its entire fortune to the senior line.

## Proposed institutions and financial governance — for further approval

- **Compagnia Patrimoniale Valdieri:** senior-owned capital/stewardship platform supervised through the Steward of Assets, not universal ownership of cadet funds.
- **Officine Valdieri Group:** the principal senior industrial platform, possibly employing Matteo in a senior technical role without making him chief executive merely because of birth.
- **Fondazione Valdieri per il Patrimonio e le Scienze Applicate:** a true nonprofit with its own mission and property rules, not a casually liquidated House bank account.
- **Archivio della Rocca / Ufficio della Continuità:** protected constitutional records and independent continuity infrastructure; their operating budgets do not become personal family wealth.
- **Consorzio Tecnico Valdieri:** cooperative training and supplier-support institution spanning separate owners; partnership participation is not a merged balance sheet.
- **Genova and Canavese ventures:** remain independently chartered and must eventually have their own governance and financial registers.
