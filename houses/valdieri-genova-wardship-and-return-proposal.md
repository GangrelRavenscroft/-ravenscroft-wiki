# House Valdieri — The Genova Settlement: Wardship and Return — LOCKED

**LOCKED CANON — October 2026.** This document establishes the general Genova household settlement, normal return ages 21–23, staged reconnection from the mid-teens, induction around 18 and deeper formation around 21, and the private Atto di Rientro return ceremony. No individual child, number, later life, marriage, career, or descendants are assigned by this document.

## What is already established

At the end of the 1934–1946 Valdieri dynastic civil war, the senior line defeats the rebel Monferrato cadet branch; the **entire Monferrato bloodline is extinguished**. No Monferrato children survive and none are concealed under other family identities. Genova, which backed Monferrato, survives after the censure of its leadership, restitution and the compulsory placement of selected **Genova** children within the principal House household. Canavese, initially neutral, ultimately supported the senior line.

The children placed with the mainline are **acknowledged as Genova by blood** and raised as relatives, not disguised as children of the principal branch. Their transfer is publicly presented as dynastic reconciliation and elite family formation, while secretly serving as a guarantee that Genova's leadership remains compliant. They may receive serious education and later pursue good professional positions that contribute to the House, but access to important confidential offices or sensitive institutions is controlled. **Their personal biographies, marriages, adult lives, exact number and names are intentionally unresolved.**

## Established duration and principle

**Normal exit from principal-house residential guardianship: about age 21–23 (locked).** This fits the Order's induction framework: initial induction around 18; more demanding formation from approximately 21. The children do not all return in the same calendar year, because they enter at different ages. Return is also not a one-day termination of all House obligations.

This is the specific historical Genova settlement pattern, **not a mandatory Order rule for all children**. A particular ward might return younger or older for education or family reasons when later defined, but we should not assign individual exceptions yet.

## The 1947–1948 settlement and its review — established period

**1947–1948:** The formal *Household Reconciliation Settlement* transfers guardianship/residence of some Genova children to the principal line, without changing their acknowledged blood descent. The settlement is publicly explained by education, reconciliation and protection after a civil war; restricted annexes clarify the political surety.

**1953–1956:** After adoption of the Order-wide **Articles of Dynastic Continuity**, Valdieri reviews the wardship agreements to ensure that modern recognized succession records, House identity and future adult return arrangements do not produce fresh competing claims. The Compact-wide Articles do not openly mandate taking children, and do not themselves legitimize the confidential surety practice.

**Late 1950s–1960s:** The wards leave principal-house residential guardianship in phases as they mature. The historical existence of this phased return can be established without assigning a specific return year, profession, marriage, or fate to any one child.

## The Atto di Rientro — formal adult return

The return is not a public emancipation from imprisonment: both families have maintained for years that these are loved and exceptionally educated Valdieri children. The settlement therefore uses **a dignified household return rather than a court-like release**. The family ceremony is called the *Atto di Rientro* (Act of Return).

A formal dinner at the ancestral estate and a subsequent reception with the Genova household would emphasize kinship. The mainline Head acknowledges the end of residential guardianship and the adult's continuing membership in the greater dynasty. Genova's Head affirms adherence to the current succession and cadet charter. The adult retains his or her real Genova identity, personal property and ordinary adult choices, without an automatic promotion in House security or sensitive office.

To avoid turning them into static political symbols, no personal outcomes should be assigned here. The experience could produce a range of attitudes, but whether any given former ward is loyal, resentful, politically active, or indifferent remains **unwritten**.

## Why the children do not simply remain forever

Permanently retaining adult relatives against their wishes would defeat the settlement's stated purpose and could deepen Genova resentment. It would also expose Valdieri to further constitutional and political challenges after its degradation. A system that gradually returns adults, having built years of genuine family relationships, gives the senior line a credible long-term result without requiring an endlessly expanding group of wards.

The coercive reality can nevertheless be remembered. The adults may become bridges between households without having been free to choose their original residence. That contradiction is central to Valdieri's political culture.

## Controls and privileges after return — established generalities

The postwar relationship rests on identifiable constitutional succession, restricted powers over common assets, defined branch charters, credit and investment interdependence, access-based appointment practices, and continuing personal relationships with the mainline. None of these should imply that every member is surveilled constantly or that a born Genova member is guilty of the rebellion. Ordinary education and careers are not automatically denied.

**Explicit distinction:** The 2026 marriage of Giulia Valdieri to Elias Ravenscroft is a legitimate adult marriage. Its private dynastic meaning parallels inter-House surety, but it is not governed by the 1947 Genova wardship settlement and does not convey Valdieri sovereignty or property to Elias.
