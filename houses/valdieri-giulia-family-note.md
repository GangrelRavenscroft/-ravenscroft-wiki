# House Valdieri — Giulia Caterina Valdieri (2026 family note)

**Status:** Established character and marriage direction; dates and personal details in development.

Giulia Caterina Valdieri is approximately 22 in 2026, a younger daughter of the reigning Head of the recently restored Great House Valdieri. Although formerly placed high in its succession, she has married Elias Alexander Ravenscroft (born December 6, 1989; age 36), the youngest son of Charles William Ravenscroft and Naomi Katherine Sloane. Elias is a comparatively junior Ravenscroft blood relative specializing in quantum and advanced computing.

The alliance was deliberately asymmetrical. Valdieri sought the prestige and credibility of marriage into the most powerful House, since other Great Houses barely respect its newly recovered standing. Ravenscroft offered a genuine alliance but not a principal heir.

Giulia willingly relinquished her Valdieri succession rights for herself and descendants through her. She keeps her blood identity and personal rights. Elias acquires no Valdieri Headship claim, governing office, or control of Valdieri resources. Although the match costs Giulia her birthright, her personal standing rises as a Ravenscroft spouse.

Elias does not know the role Ravenscroft covertly played in Valdieri's postwar downfall; his place and clearance in Ravenscroft are too junior for that knowledge.
