## Established historical context

The historically powerful Monferrato cadet branch initiated the civil war, backed by Genova under Raffaele Benedetto Valdieri di Genova (1887–1964). Under Adelaide Teresa Valdieri di Canavese (1892–1973), Canavese began neutral and later aided the principal line. The Monferrato bloodline was completely extinguished, leaving no children or hidden descendants. Genova survived but received censure, restitution and the requirement that selected children be raised in the principal household as family wards and implicit political guarantees. Their particular identities, adult careers and life courses remain **intentionally unassigned**. Canavese helped the principal line preserve necessary assets and institutions. Lorenzo Vittorio Valdieri recovered Great-House standing in 2023.

The broad outline should not make every Genova descendant a villain or every Canavese descendant loyal. No one living in 2026 needs to inherit a fixed moral identity from an ancestor's choice. Some relationships have healed; some have not.

## I. Surviving House Valdieri di Genova — established

### Claudia Renata Valdieri di Genova (born 1964; age 62)
**Position:** Current Head of the Genova cadet branch. Apparent age about 52–56, reflecting Valdieri's somewhat less pronounced longevity compared with elite Ravenscroft blood.

**Profession:** Independent chair of the branch's legitimate commercial investments, specialized transport-infrastructure interests, institutional lending and heritage-property stakes. This is not control of a Veyrholm-style shipping empire: Genova's historical strength lies in maritime-connected finance, contracts, and commercial institutions rather than all Mediterranean ports.

**Personality:** Exceptionally composed, meticulous, proud, slow to trust the senior line but rational about Valdieri's precarious restored status. She wants full respect for the lawful boundaries of cadet resources and believes the restoration would be endangered by another politically motivated confiscation.

**Wartime relationship:** Descendant of the surviving Genova branch; exact genealogical connection to Raffaele is still open and does not presume she was descended from a child raised as a political ward.

**Modern tension:** Supports the House's 2023 elevation while believing Lorenzo's senior line receives too much credit for decades of cadet contributions. She respects Matteo's practical reform approach more than Lorenzo's emphasis on prestige, but she would never openly campaign against a legitimate Head.

### Federico Alessio Valdieri di Genova (born 1992; age 34)
**Role:** Claudia's son, a specialist surgeon or medical researcher based in northern Italy; avoids branch finance. Independently accomplished and connected to the wider Valdieri family, but does not receive sensitive constitutional authority merely by virtue of being a cadet relative.

### Elisa Beatrice Valdieri di Genova (born 1998; age 28)
**Role:** Claudia's daughter, a heritage-conservation and cultural-institution professional who helps recover, document and restore the family's surviving cultural assets. She may be unusually sympathetic to Giulia's preference for a self-directed life despite a costly dynastic marriage.

**Marriage:** Claudia's spouse and her children's other parent remains to be designed rather than giving this family generic background relations.

**Institutional function in 2026:** Significant contributor of expertise and operating assets, but its finances, security and succession powers are precisely chartered. Its dignity has gradually recovered since the war, though some historical restrictions and distrust persist.

## II. Surviving House Valdieri di Canavese — established

### Tommaso Enrico Valdieri di Canavese (born 1967; age 59)
**Position:** Current Head of the Canavese cadet branch. Apparent age about 49–53.

**Profession:** Mechanical/industrial engineer and chairman of family-linked specialist manufacturing, materials and production facilities in northern Italy; hands-on technical background and an earned reputation with the professional managers who keep Valdieri productive.

**Personality:** Direct, pragmatic, less courtly than Claudia, generally aligned with Matteo's insistence that the House must first remain sustainable. Descended from the surviving Canavese line historically led by Adelaide Teresa, but the exact relationship and intermediate generations remain to be developed.

**Modern tension:** He considers the sacrifice involved in Giulia's marriage understandable yet regards overreliance on Ravenscroft as potentially dangerous. He is loyal to House Valdieri's independence rather than personally obedient to Lorenzo in all circumstances.

### Lucia Teresa Valdieri di Canavese (born 1995; age 31)
**Role:** Tommaso's daughter, materials scientist involved in applied research and industrial product development. A professional collaborator with Matteo, sometimes a friendly competitor, not a political rival for the senior Headship.

### Pietro Davide Valdieri di Canavese (born 2001; age 25)
**Role:** Tommaso's son, ecologist and environmental land-management specialist associated with productive rural property and biodiversity/land rehabilitation. Represents House continuity outside the banking-and-boardroom stereotype.

**Marriage:** Tommaso's spouse and the children's other parent remain unassigned for now.

**Institutional function in 2026:** Canavese has particularly credible technical personnel and productive assets. It was a dependable reconstruction partner but must still operate within explicit House-level cadet authority; support does not entitle it to unilateral control of the senior succession.

## IV. Established 2026 family council and restoration governance

**Established constitutional structure.** The seven-seat **Consiglio di Casa Valdieri** would be a distinct House governing council, not one of the nine International Council mandates.

| Seat | Proposed holder | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| House Head | Lorenzo Vittorio Valdieri | Final dynastic authority and Conclave Voice; succession acts remain bound by the charter |
| Heir | Matteo Lorenzo Valdieri | Strategic operations, technical institutional development and continuity |
| Senior Consort | Dr. Elena Beatrice Montanari Valdieri | Medical, educational and family formation oversight, with full vote by competence and appointment rather than merely marriage |
| Genova Cadet Voice | Claudia Renata Valdieri di Genova | Branch charter representation, commercial assets and protections |
| Canavese Cadet Voice | Tommaso Enrico Valdieri di Canavese | Industrial continuity and branch charter representation |
| Steward of Assets | Officeholder to assign | Independently auditable financial and property reserves |
| Warden of Constitutional Continuity | Officeholder to assign | Secure succession register, restricted archives, emergency continuity, branch mandate verification |

**Governing rule:** Routine council recommendations need a simple majority, but changes affecting the senior succession, cadet privileges, entire-House security chain or structural assets require five of seven approvals and proper assent of the lawful Head, subject to the Compact. Neither cadet voice is permitted to claim a separate Conclave Voice. No council vote can grant Giulia's husband power over Valdieri, undo her renunciation without constitutional revision, or displace the recognized succession by a side agreement.

**Role of Giulia and Chiara:** Giulia keeps blood kinship and diplomatic standing but is excluded from constitutional succession following her marriage. The council's sensitive voting and institutional access are not inherited automatically by any spouse. Chiara is formally second successor but, being eighteen in 2026, is not yet assigned full institutional command or voting authority simply because of her place in line.

**Why the system works:** 2023 recognition depends on proving this House can govern independently under its own law, maintain essential institutions when one branch dissents, and keep a legitimate line of command clear. The two cadet voices are included in normal governance while neither can seize institutions under a rival charter.

**Political consequence:** Lorenzo's council would contain competing viewpoints but no credible succession ambiguity. Matteo and Tommaso might form a practical reform bloc, Elena may favor personal welfare over status, and Claudia may press for cadet dignity and fair accounting without reviving rebel claims. No obligation to make every disagreement into conspiracy.
