# Valdieri — The 1946–2023 Cadet-Rank Transition

**STATUS: APPROVED AND LOCKED CONSTITUTIONAL CANON — October 11, 2026.** This supplements the locked 2026 structure and does not change the established historical war, restoration or household identities.

## The contradiction we must address

Valdieri was historically a Great House, but following the 1934–1946 civil war its international Great-House status and Conclave Voice were revoked and it stood as a **Major House** until its re-elevation in **2023**. In 2026, both surviving cadet Houses, **Valdieri di Genova** and **Valdieri di Canavese**, are recognized as **Major-ranked cadets** which directly answer to the restored sovereign Great House.

The now-established rank rule is that **a Major House cannot be the constitutional subordinate of another Major House**. We therefore must not silently write the 1946–2023 cadet branches as *fully recognized independent Major-rank Houses formally subordinated to a Major-rank principal Valdieri* throughout that period.

## Ratified reconciliation — dormant external rank and internal cadet authority

**Ratified canon:** During the demotion, the historic cadet family identities, private assets, estates, succession practice and household leadership continue. Their **external House-rank standing as separate regional Major Houses is suspended or unrecognized under the postwar settlement**. They function as recognized **internal cadet divisions of the demoted Valdieri Major House**, administering their own patrimony and businesses under the former Great House's residual family compact rather than two independent internationally acknowledged Major sovereignties.

This is a **temporary constitutional anomaly**, not a general exception that lets Major Houses rule other Major Houses. It reflects the special punitive reduction of an old Great House rather than the creation of a new subordinate order.

- **1946–1953:** The demotion and Genova's punitive settlement create restricted and disputed branch statuses. Canavese preserves much of its ordinary practical autonomy; Genova is censured and subject to restitution and family wardship. Neither cadet has an independent international Conclave Voice.
- **1953–1956 Articles of Dynastic Continuity:** Surviving cadet charters and succession instruments are authenticated and administratively stabilized. The Articles do not grant Valdieri lost Great-House rank and do not make its subdivisions full peer-level subordinate Major Houses under another Major.
- **1956–2022:** Genova and Canavese function as internally recognized branches with their own households and separate private property. They may be socially styled as *cadet Houses* but **not fully externally recognized as a pair of Major Houses formally subordinate to Major Valdieri**. Their internal functions can resemble regional Major Houses without changing international constitutional rank.
- **2023 re-elevation:** Valdieri regains sovereign Great-House status; its restored constitutional authority permits the **formal reinstatement or acknowledgment of Major-House standing for Genova and Canavese**, each now directly answerable to Lorenzo as sovereign Great-House Head.
- **2024–2026:** Other independent-origin Major dynasties, such as Roccabruna and Marenesi, may negotiate direct Great-House charters. Their proposed exact binding dates remain to be reviewed; they did **not** answer constitutionally to Valdieri when Valdieri itself was a Major House.

The internal cadet family identity and property continuity are never extinguished by this model; only externally acknowledged rank and sovereign subordination require careful historical distinctions.

## Rejected alternative retained as a development note

The rejected alternative would have held that during 1946–2023 the cadets could have been **separately independent, recognized Major Houses** with elective allied status to the demoted senior family, not formal subordinates. Their allegiance could only be made legally binding upon the Great-House restoration. This offers more autonomy for Genova but weakens the existing history of enforced postwar penalties and family wardship unless those are separately explained as the results of negotiated surrender and internal family law.

## Ratified canon outcome

Keep Genova and Canavese as **distinct blood cadet identities with their own genuine property and internal Headships during the demotion**, while **externally dormant or downgraded in formal Major-ranking constitutional status**. In **2023**, as part of Valdieri's restoration, both cadet Houses regain or receive formal Major-House regional rank. They subsequently answer **directly to sovereign Great House Valdieri** and retain the distinct 2026 wealth that is already locked.

This preserves the rank rule, postwar sanctions, adult return of Genova wards and the significance of the 2023 restoration. **The dormant-rank interpretation was expressly approved by the user and is locked.** The precise 2023 charter instruments and signatures may be developed later; no part of this establishes Major-over-Major constitutional subordination.
