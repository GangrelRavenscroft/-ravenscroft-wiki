# Valdieri Regional Authority and Major-House Charters — CANON

**STATUS: LOCKED VALDIERI REGIONAL CANON — October 2026.** The four broad primary regional assignments, five Lesser-House local/specialist mandates, principle of commercial overlap without superior constitutional authority, senior-House direct estates, and the Major-to-Great appeal structure are established. **Still open:** precise boundary lines, special districts, charter signature/effective dates, procedural reporting frequencies, named Lesser Heads, detailed corporate holdings and individual family biographies.

## Constitutional rule — established

A **Great House** is sovereign in its House network. Every subordinate **Major House** is a **regional authority**, answerable directly to the Great House. A **blood cadet** may be Major-ranked or Lesser-ranked and receives additional dynastic privileges, but “cadet” is not a rank between Great and Major. A **Lesser House** serves either a defined **local jurisdiction** or a **specialist industry/profession** (or both). A Major may supervise Lesser Houses; it cannot be the constitutional superior of another Major.

House government is not state government. A House's jurisdiction determines its duties and rights within the hidden Order, not civil ownership of a city, all public officials or every enterprise inside that area. A family can have businesses outside its assigned House region. Their ownership continues to follow real beneficial title.

## III. Boundaries, exceptions, and authority conflicts

### A. The difference between a region and a monopoly

A regional Major oversees the **relationships and obligations of House-aligned institutions** recognized within its charter. It does not automatically own the commercial economy, local government, ports, banking, courts, schools or every patron in the area. Civil authorities and independent competitors retain agency.

### B. Major versus Major

Majors at the same rank cannot command one another, regardless of relative wealth or bloodline. A Genova/Marenesi commercial dispute is handled commercially where possible; a dynastic dispute over charter obligations is elevated to the Great House. An expert House may be hired by a peer and advise it without acquiring authority over it.

### C. Direct Lesser versus regional Major

The principal Great House may reserve specialist or protected local mandates directly, even if their premises lie in a Major's normal region. This **does not subordinate the Major** to the Lesser or vice versa. Roccabruna and Serravento can share territory for different defined missions; formal supervision follows their charters.

### D. Cadet family privileges

Genova and Canavese retain kinship rights, family formation, eligibility for specified dynastic events and offices, and special obligations of the senior-line blood network. Roccabruna and Marenesi need not receive identical privileges simply because all four possess Major rank. Distinguish protocol honor, wealth, branch legal rights, and operational remit.

### E. Regional versus sovereign House authority

Lorenzo keeps overarching House obligations, Conclave Voice, the principal House estates and protected constitutional instruments. The **Consiglio di Casa Valdieri** assists under its locked seven-seat structure and protected five-of-seven plus Head concurrence requirement. The council does not possess one voting seat for each non-blood Major merely because all Majors report directly to the sovereign Great House; the previously ratified composition stays unchanged.

## IV. How the system functions day to day — examples for future dossiers

- **Industrial facility near Ivrea:** Canavese handles regional House relationships; Valcerra may supply standards work, but individual corporations still have their own management and ownership.
- **Bridge and watershed work around Cuneo:** Roccabruna leads the assigned regional House liaison. Serravento may exercise its direct senior-House local mission where an estate or protected site is implicated, requiring coordination rather than forced hierarchy.
- **Marine-insurance dispute involving Genoa and La Spezia:** Genova and Marenesi can separately represent their own institutions and agree on a joint commercial position. Neither can assume superiority over the other, even if one House is wealthier.
- **Succession or archive emergency at Rocca delle Fonti:** the Warden of Constitutional Continuity (office established; incumbent unnamed) acts within the principal House mandate. Regional Majors may assist but cannot displace the constitutional archive or create a rival Headship.
