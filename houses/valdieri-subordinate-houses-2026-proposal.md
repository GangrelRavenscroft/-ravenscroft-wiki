## 1. The corrected constitutional hierarchy

**A cadet House is a blood-related kind of House, not a separate constitutional rank inserted between Great and Major Houses.** A cadet House may operate at **Major-House standing** or **Lesser-House standing**, depending upon the actual charter, resources and status. As a rule, **every Major House—whether a blood cadet or a distinct non-blood dynasty—owes its direct House-level allegiance to the sovereign Great House**. A Major House may supervise Lesser Houses within delegated authority, but may not interpose itself as the constitutional superior of another Major House.

House Valdieri di Genova and House Valdieri di Canavese are both **cadet Houses of Major-House standing** in 2026. They possess separate Heads, household institutions, succession, real wealth, professional staff and lesser dependencies, together with additional Valdieri blood-family privileges and responsibilities. Neither cadet receives a private Conclave Voice or an international Council mandate. They answer to Lorenzo as the Head of sovereign House Valdieri, just as do the non-blood Major Houses Roccabruna and Marenesi.

The principal House's approved net worth is **US$3.5–5B**. House Valdieri di Genova separately holds **$1–2B**, and House Valdieri di Canavese separately holds **$1.5–2.5B**. Other subordinate Houses' own property is excluded from all three.

A Major's region means responsibility for the House's relationships, chartered obligations, regional security and internal network—not ownership of a province, its government or every business.

## 5. Chain of authority and dispute routes

- **Great to Major:** Lorenzo or the duly delegated central House authority speaks directly to the Heads of Genova, Canavese, Roccabruna and Marenesi on matters lawfully reserved to the Great House. Major Houses manage ordinary internal personnel, investments, households and external relations themselves.
- **Major to Lesser:** Claudia exercises chartered superior authority over Portelivo, while Tommaso does so over Valcerra and Boscariva. These are genuine Lesser-House dependencies, not corporate subsidiaries or a claim over every privately held asset.
- **Direct Lesser allegiance:** Belmonte d'Orta and Serravento have constitutional relationships directly with the Great House, bypassing no Major House because none is their immediate superior.
- **Commercial networks:** Genova and Marenesi can coordinate maritime and insurance projects through contracts, co-investment or mutual patronage. Such commercial partnerships do not create a constitutional superior-subordinate relationship.
- **Appeals and mediation:** Disputes over a chartered Lesser House's obligations ordinarily begin with its immediate superior; matters involving contested constitutional authority, threatened whole-House continuity, Compact obligations or conflicts between peer-level Major Houses may rise to the senior Great-House system.
- **Protected assets:** The parent House's superior constitutional rank does not automatically convey full beneficial ownership of subordinate Houses' private assets. All approved independent net worths remain separate.

## 6. What each tier contributes

**Principal House:** protected sovereignty, international peer recognition, central constitutional legitimacy, long-horizon capital, industrial coordination and the restored Great-House seal.

**Genova — cadet Major:** independent Ligurian commerce, capital, heritage-property networks and a subordinate Lesser House in Portelivo; historically uneasy about central interference after its civil-war punishment.

**Canavese — cadet Major:** substantive industrial and technical assets, ecology and land interests, with two Lesser Houses under its own authority; often closely aligned with Matteo's practical professional outlook.

**Roccabruna — non-blood Major:** engineering, infrastructure resilience, technical assessment and regional administrative relationships; its own wealth and Headship survive independently.

**Marenesi — non-blood Major:** merchant-finance and marine-risk systems with a separate direct allegiance to the Great House. Its strong business dealings with Genova do **not** place it below Genova in rank.

**Lesser Houses:** dependable local services, estates, workforce, professional know-how and reputational connections. Some command more influence in a narrow territory than their wealth implies.

## 7. Political tensions appropriate to a restored Great House

Lorenzo can be proud of binding Roccabruna and Marenesi while preserving intact cadet institutions. That is real diplomatic success for a House that lost its standing for generations.

Claudia must respect Marenesi as a **separate Major-House peer** despite both dynasties' Genoese interests. She may prefer a direct commercial alliance and can resent central interference, but her personal stature and independent cadet wealth remain substantial.

Tommaso retains direct chartered oversight of Valcerra and Boscariva as their Major-standing superior. Other Major Houses cannot commandeer them through commerce alone.

Matteo may form professional alliances with Roccabruna, Canavese and Valcerra, while still lacking the right to command an independent Major House merely because he is heir. Elena, Giulia and Chiara retain their established identities, responsibilities and limits.

No part of this hierarchy revives the extinct Monferrato bloodline, changes the Genova wardship return canon, or implies that external organized crime or Italian government institutions belong to Valdieri without separate evidence.
