# House Valdieri — Bound Major Houses and Lesser Houses (2026)

**STATUS: NEW SUBORDINATE-HOUSE DEVELOPMENT FOR REVIEW — NOT LOCKED.** This document introduces *candidate* non-blood subordinate dynasties and their individual Heads for review. Their names, births, net worths, binding dates, offices and territories are **not established** unless subsequently approved. The Valdieri estates, institutions and council charters are already LOCKED. Preserve the separate Great-House and cadet-House wealth bands.

## 1. The three-house core is established

The principal sovereign Great House is headed by **Lorenzo Vittorio Valdieri**. Its own operations include Rocca delle Fonti (constitutional seat), Palazzo Valdieri in Milan (commercial seat), Compagnia Patrimoniale Valdieri, Officine Valdieri, Fondazione Valdieri, Archivio della Rocca / Ufficio della Continuità and Consorzio Tecnico Valdieri. The two cadet Houses are **distinct subordinate Houses**, not departments:

- **House Valdieri di Genova**, headed by **Claudia Renata Valdieri di Genova**, owns its own Palazzo di Levante and Compagnia di Levante Valdieri, and preserves independent commercial, financial and heritage-property operations.
- **House Valdieri di Canavese**, headed by **Tommaso Enrico Valdieri di Canavese**, owns its own Villa della Rocchetta and Tecnologie Canavese Valdieri, and controls separate industrial, technical and productive-land operations.

The principal House has **US$3.5–5B** in attributable net wealth in 2026, **Genova US$1–2B**, and **Canavese US$1.5–2.5B**. None of the wealth independently owned by their subordinate non-blood Major or Lesser Houses is counted in these figures.

**Suggested scale:** two genuinely significant *bound Major Houses* and five narrower *Lesser Houses*. This is sufficient to demonstrate functional sovereignty and subordinate networks without falsely turning weakened Valdieri into a global conglomerate. The political benefits can exceed their monetary value.

**Distinct relationships:** each subordinate House possesses its own blood family, succession, Head, property, income, employees and professional clients. Cadets enjoy special shared Valdieri blood privileges and carry specific dynastic obligations; bound Major and Lesser Houses do not become blood Valdieri simply by allegiance. The Head of a bound House retains actual internal authority rather than becoming Lorenzo's employee.

The senior Great House reserves defined inter-House obligations, Conclave representation, continuity review, common strategic coordination and escalation of serious disputes. Most routine subordinate House matters are handled by the immediate superior—**not by bypassing Claudia or Tommaso to seek directions directly from Lorenzo**.

## 3. Bound Major House Roccabruna — DIRECT to principal House (proposal)

- **Fictional identity:** *Casa Roccabruna*, rooted in southwest Piedmont, with professional headquarters in the Turin–Cuneo corridor.
- **Current proposed Head:** **Dr. Beatrice Ludovica Roccabruna**, born 1969, age 57 in 2026; civil engineer with a hydrogeology and regional-infrastructure background.
- **Proposed independent net worth:** **US$500–800 million**, excluding all Valdieri wealth. This is a Major House's own patrimony, not another component of Lorenzo's $3.5–5B.
- **Real power:** specialist engineering institutions, transport and utility resilience consulting, protected industrial-site inspections, ownership stakes in regional civil works, and trusted professional relationships with municipal administrations and insurers.
- **Basis of allegiance:** postwar recognition of the principal Valdieri Head, mutual protection, retained internal Headship and a chartered technical-service obligation. Roccabruna is valuable because it can do things a financier cannot—understand, build and preserve real infrastructure.
- **Historic tension:** its predecessor generation defended critical property and infrastructure during Valdieri's recovery but resisted being absorbed into the principal commercial group. In 2026 its Head works well with Matteo but demands competent professional decisions over prestige projects.
- **Boundary:** regional political access and engineering contracts do not make Roccabruna the secret owner of Piedmontese government or utilities.

## 4. Bound Major House Marenesi — UNDER GENOVA (proposal)

- **Fictional identity:** *Casa Marenesi*, based in Genoa and the Ligurian commercial sphere, with a separate merchant-house lineage.
- **Current proposed Head:** **Giorgio Pietro Marenesi**, born 1970, age 56; formerly active in maritime insurance, commercial risk and institutional finance.
- **Proposed independent net worth:** **US$350–650 million**, not included in Genova's $1–2B, and not included in the principal Great House's wealth.
- **Real power:** specialist marine commercial insurance services, bonded commercial property, risk assessments, finance-adjacent services, and durable relationships with port-area businesses and international insurers.
- **Basis of allegiance:** treaty and charter obligations to **Cadet House Genova**. Claudia exercises the immediate bound-House relationship; Lorenzo's Great House has only the higher-level powers reserved by the Genova charter and common Valdieri law.
- **Historic tension:** wartime and postwar Genova insecurity teaches Marenesi not to build all its prosperity around one Cadet Head. Its leadership favors the restored Great House's stability but negotiates protected independence in business operations.
- **Boundary:** no monopoly on Mediterranean maritime commerce, ports or underwriters; other Great Houses and independent institutions remain important.

## 6. How authority would move through the network

**Ordinary administration:** Portelivo's Head answers to its own household and to Claudia on the obligations of the Genova charter, not directly to Lorenzo about every customer or employee. Valcerra and Boscariva likewise answer first to Canavese for cadet-tier commitments. Senior-line bound Houses answer through Lorenzo's competent House office, probably a future secretary or liaison under the Consiglio, without turning the Head into every firm's CEO.

**Serious protected decisions:** A subordinate House proposing to transfer important common obligations, repudiate parent-House allegiance, displace a lawful Head or expose confidential Order matters triggers the immediate superior's charter and, if necessary, principal Great-House review. Constitutional review does not automatically transfer private property title.

**Political and local influence:** Most regional commercial, educational, industrial or civic relationships are maintained by these Heads and their professional networks. The principal House coordinates national or international priorities only where its real institutional reach warrants it, avoiding an implausible claim to dominate all Italian government.

**Financial influence:** The principal House can offer access to its technical consortium, long-horizon credit and respected professional network. Genova and Canavese can each offer comparable opportunities within their own spheres. Benefits are conditional upon legitimate relationships and negotiated charters; no House automatically owns the assets of a subordinate simply for providing financing.

**Covert responsibilities:** Nothing here declares these subordinate families an intelligence service or criminal syndicate. Specific darker Valdieri activities require later canon decisions. Rebuilding a functioning, lawful dynastic hierarchy after the Monferrato catastrophe is itself a source of status and power.

## 7. Why this does not recreate the civil war

Roccabruna and Marenesi possess real wealth and important skills but not Monferrato's former independent claim to Great-House recognition, massive embedded resources or wartime support base. Lesser Houses are professionally valuable but cannot easily constitute a rival Valdieri sovereign command.

The postwar **Articles of Dynastic Continuity**, specific cadet charters, known succession, council voting safeguards, distinct beneficial-ownership records and the greater House's intergenerational educational and economic relationships reduce—not eliminate—succession vulnerability. A subordinate House could be discontented or attempt separation, but it would need real long-duration independence and formal recognition to become an independent constitutional peer.

The **Genova wartime wards** are not a general model for relationships with Roccabruna or Marenesi. The former settlement arose from explicit censure of a rebel-allied cadet and remains a morally complicated historical exception.

## 8. Political balance in 2026

- **Lorenzo:** values order, clear charters and the diplomatic respect of being restored to the Great Houses.
- **Matteo:** wants technical competence and viable businesses; may find allies among Roccabruna, Valcerra and Canavese.
- **Claudia:** supports the wider dynasty but insists Genova's Marenesi and Portelivo relationships are **her branch's** constitutional responsibilities, not simply Lorenzo's personal assets.
- **Tommaso:** values the technical productivity of Valcerra and ecological competence of Boscariva, and sees durable industrial operations as the true safeguard against another collapse.
- **Elena:** may have charitable and educational relationships with these families, distinct from commanding them financially or constitutionally.
- **Giulia:** her marriage provides important Ravenscroft connections but does not transfer subordinate Valdieri control to Elias.
- **Chiara:** can know some younger technical professionals socially or through training without being assigned an operational post at age eighteen.
