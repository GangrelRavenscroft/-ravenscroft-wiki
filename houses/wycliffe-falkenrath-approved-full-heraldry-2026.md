Midnight/navy-blue shield; a sheer ivory-white cliff or escarpment topped by a golden stone tower above stylized blue-and-gold water. Two silver/ivory griffin supporters with gold accents. Two prominent heraldic falcon supporters, gilded mantling in black/crimson/gold, princely ornamentation and a falcon crest above the helm. This replaces the older full arms in the House masthead, full-heraldry hero and House visual-canon gallery. Existing history, House offices, titles and the page's established motto are unchanged.
