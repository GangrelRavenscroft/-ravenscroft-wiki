Institutional Framework

An international structure distinct from any one House

The Order includes multiple Great Houses and senior offices. House governance, Order office, blood status, membership, knowledge, clearance, compartment access, patronage, wealth, and operational command are deliberately separate concepts.

Induction

Ravenscroft blood members normally receive initial induction around age eighteen and deeper adult formation around age twenty-one, with later rites, warrants, and compartment access determined individually.

Governance

International governance includes a nine-seat Council and senior offices across Houses and regions. No single Great House is synonymous with the Order.

Clearance

Security clearance and compartment access are not the same thing. A member may have high clearance in a specialized compartment without broad operational authority elsewhere.

Authority

Lethal, paramilitary, intelligence, patronage, educational, financial, and social-development authority are tracked separately where relevant.
