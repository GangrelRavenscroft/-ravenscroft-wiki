Party Temperament

Alexandra remains a genuine party girl in 2026, though less relentlessly than during her undergraduate and early graduate-school years. She likes crowded rooms, loud music, dancing, flirtation, late-night conversation, spontaneous after-parties, football weekends, destination trips, and the small dramas that form around socially ambitious people.

She does not merely tolerate these environments for professional reasons; she thrives in them. That natural comfort is one reason the sorority and alumnae-development portfolio fits her so well. She understands the social world from inside it rather than observing it as an outsider.

Responsibility & Excess

Alexandra is capable of being highly responsible and can become serious almost instantly when work, family, or genuine danger requires it. She protects teaching obligations, important meetings, confidential responsibilities, and other people's safety.

She is not perfectly self-regulating, however. When she feels genuinely off-duty, she can stay out far later than intended, drink beyond her planned limit, make impulsive decisions, or turn a good night into an excessive one simply because she is having too much fun to stop.

Alcohol & Recreational Use

Alcohol is a normal part of Alexandra's social life. She drinks moderately most of the time and has a developed taste for wine, champagne, and cocktails, but occasional nights of real intoxication are part of her history and have not disappeared completely by 2026.

She also has occasional recreational substance use in adult social settings. It is not portrayed as habitual dependence or central to her identity; the greater character point is that she is pleasure-seeking, socially adventurous, and sometimes willing to push beyond her usual restraint.

Sexuality & Flirtation

Alexandra is openly sex-positive, flirtatious, and comfortable with consensual adult sexual freedom. Marriage does not make her pretend attraction to other people has disappeared, and the openness of her relationship with Evan was established from the beginning.

Her lack of prudishness is another reason younger adult women in the sorority network often find her easy to talk to. She can discuss attraction, hookups, jealousy, relationships, sexual mistakes, boundaries, and reputation without moral panic. That comfort supports trust, but formal advancement decisions remain subject to conflict safeguards and recusal.

Hosting at Lindenmere

Alexandra enjoys elegant dinners, but Lindenmere is equally known for parties that become larger and later than planned. A small dinner can become drinks in the library, music on the terrace, people around the pool, unexpected arrivals, and a last cluster of guests still talking in the kitchen well after midnight.

University and Alabama football weekends are especially social. Visiting alumnae, friends, partners, and family may cycle through the house across an entire weekend rather than attend one formal event.

Private Rhythm

Left to herself, Alexandra is not a morning person. After a late Saturday she is perfectly capable of treating noon as breakfast. Quieter recovery days tend toward coffee, reading, riding, the garden or pool, takeout or an easy meal with Evan and Riley, and deliberately avoiding unnecessary appointments.

Her recurring ordinary flaw is that “one more drink and then we're leaving” is not a reliable forecast. Evan in particular has learned to interpret it generously.

Wealth & Taste

Alexandra genuinely enjoys luxury and does not perform embarrassment about it: clothes, jewelry, restaurants, travel, beautiful rooms, good alcohol, horses, and memorable experiences all appeal to her. She prefers quality and atmosphere over conspicuous logo-driven display and dislikes wealth used solely to demand deference.

She is generous more naturally through experiences, access, introductions, gifts, and opportunities than through austere financial discipline. Practical household administration is something she is happy to delegate.

Personal Philosophy

People should be free to want what they want—but they should understand why they want it.

That belief links Alexandra's academic interests, sexual openness, mentoring style, and instinctive fascination with social behavior. She does not regard desire as shameful; she regards unexamined desire as dangerous.

Alexandra's nightlife, sexuality, and appetite for pleasure are real parts of her character rather than a cover or a youthful phase. They coexist with genuine professional discipline, intellectual seriousness, family loyalty, and the capacity to become highly focused when circumstances demand it.
