### Relaxed geographic quality standard — user-directed

This is **a fictional in-universe intelligence atlas, not a professional geographic chart**. The map must remain geographically plausible: countries in recognizable relative locations; intelligible modern country and major city labels; major real islands/straits and broadly plausible coastlines; no glaring invented islands or absurdly displaced cities; centers and broad spheres consistent with canon. Minor coastline simplifications and approximate shaded regional boundaries are acceptable.

**A Natural Earth 1:10m GIS rebuild is NOT required for this narrative release.** The older independent GIS-based map (European REV10) used Natural Earth 1:110m land masks, intermediate GSHHS coastline/border overlays, ETOPO1 land relief, and EPSG:3035 geometry; it remains optional reference when greater precision is needed. The beloved final illustration itself is a standalone artwork and **is not georeferenced**, even if its geography looks convincing. On-image scale bars are decorative and do not certify distances.

### Heraldry and relative influence

The visual motif checklist is: Wycliffe cliff and tower, Veyrholm white sea-eagle, de Villiers gatehouse with dividers, Falkenrath falcon with Alps, Volkov white wolf / star / mountains / water, Valdieri bridge / star / water. The official approved original simplified crest PNGs in the House asset folders retain precedence over any artistic approximations when creating future formal heraldry.

The six House colors indicate *which* House is predominant; shade strength indicates *relative depth*. Where houses overlap, the rendering is interpretive, not a claimed physical charter boundary. Established thematic hierarchy includes prominent Oslo, Stockholm, and Gotland for Veyrholm; Salzburg/Vienna as Falkenrath's primary centers relative to Munich; predominantly western Russian/Ukraine network for Volkov rather than all of Russia; and a more limited/delegated reach for postwar Valdieri. The mapped strengths should not be treated as new independently ratified numerical canon.

### Editorial QA and status boundaries

The illustration was visually reviewed with the user and expressly approved. Compared with earlier rejected experiments it conveys Europe recognizably and does not show the conspicuous invented Mediterranean islands previously objected to. **This is qualitative screen-level checking, not a GIS or hydrographic certification**; complex coastlines and small islands are not audited individually.

The **artwork and narrative atlas plate are locked and finished**. The underlying political spheres, detailed influence values, and any real-world institutional affiliations are **not converted into hard canon** by this release. No previous official House maps were replaced. The European atlas's former second sheet of strategic/authority markers remains a research draft, not a required companion page. Further refinements are optional unless the user explicitly requests them.
