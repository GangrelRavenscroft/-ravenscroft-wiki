# European Great-House Influence Atlas — Review Model (2026)

**STATUS: DRAFT CARTOGRAPHIC PROPOSAL — NOT LOCKED GEOGRAPHIC CANON**
**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Governing canon:** [Institutional Influence & Intelligence Doctrine](institutional-influence-and-intelligence-doctrine.html).

## Continental product decision

Continue EUROPE — GREAT HOUSE INFLUENCE as the primary continental atlas sheet. The earlier EUROPE — HOUSE AUTHORITY & STRATEGIC NETWORKS map and data survive as archived working drafts, **not as a required final continental map**. The Houses have thousands of actual or potential financial, business, institutional, governmental, personnel and intelligence connections: putting each on a Europe-scale map would obscure useful geographic meaning. A future interactive city/sector atlas and institutional registers are the right place for such detail.

The existing official House influence maps in the GitHub repository remain untouched.

## Locked interpretive rules

1. **Influence differs from sovereignty, ownership, membership, clearance and intelligence coverage.** Control of a supplier, influence over financing, presence of a board protégé, personal access to a regulator and covert intelligence reporting are distinct forms of leverage.
2. **Near House cores, networks are generally denser and deeper.** Metropolises, institutional centers, universities, port hubs and industrial concentrations matter disproportionately. Remoteness does not prevent exceptional strategic nodes.
3. **More surveillance is not necessarily more control.** Independent sources can verify information and monitor loyalty; this improves situational awareness but does not confer governing authority.
4. **Rival Houses may overlap within one company or city.** Coexistence is not necessarily hostility or a constitutional dispute. Mark participants separately.
5. **The map's levels represent effective influence, not counted firms, operated cameras, employees or sworn members.** Unmapped/neutral does not mean no local influence by other Houses.
6. **The six European atlas colors and levels stay fixed.** Ravenscroft's broader European reach and unique intelligence advantages should not be silently converted into a seventh claim to European territory.

## Proposed editable data model

Keep each House × place × sector combination as independently editable records with:
- House ID, canonical name, dossier path, and legal/operational relationship classification.
- Place coordinates (with precision / approximate status) and projected geometry.
- Confirmed / inferred / proposed status, source support, review state, limitations and date.
- Level 1–5 effective institutional influence score, independently editable from spatial coordinates and spread.
- Optional qualitative network-depth/redundancy hypothesis, separate from the approved influence score.
- Optional intelligence coverage hypothesis, **not** conflated with ownership, direct authority or control.
- Sector (financial, legal, industrial, maritime, research, communications, education, government relationships, general institutional).
- Named overlap Houses and relationship classification (coexistence, negotiated jurisdiction, rivalry or active conflict).

These fields should not imply a real or fictional corporation exists where none has been documented. Hypothetical company or employee counts must never be passed off as facts.

## Map One visual revision directions (review only)

- Restore refined midnight-blue / antique-gold atlas appearance while preserving legibility.
- Use stronger city-centered peaks that taper promptly outside metropolitan centers; avoid coloring entire countries or rural provinces with the same level.
- Preserve selected distant ports, capitals and strategic institutional nodes without giving entire surrounding countryside high ratings.
- Use separate vector layers for base, borders, graded House polygons, paired/hatched overlap, labels, insets and authentic crests.
- Correct fine geography and verify cities. The previous renderer used Natural Earth 110m, not the requested 10m.
- Never invent/recreate official crest symbols; release requires all six real PNG assets embedded.

## Proposed regions requiring approval

Wycliffe — London and southeast England core, selective university/financial centers, Ireland / North Sea periphery.
Veyrholm — Gotland and Stockholm core; nuanced Baltic routes, selected Norwegian ports and Oslo; rural Norway largely neutral.
de Villiers — Burgundy and Paris core, documented Loire/Provence/Belgian/Swiss network responsibilities; Low Countries, Rhine and Iberian strength details proposed.
Falkenrath — Salzburg/Vienna/Bavaria; selective Danube and Alpine city networks rather than blanket Alpine territory.
Volkov — Western Carpathians/Lviv, Warsaw, Kyiv and documented Smolensk lines; differentiate Moscow and St Petersburg; wider Russia mostly neutral.
Valdieri — Gesso Valley, Milan, Piedmont and Liguria; weaker, more selective central Italian/Adriatic network nodes.

These geography rankings are carried forward from the original **unapproved** atlas model, not ratified by this document.
