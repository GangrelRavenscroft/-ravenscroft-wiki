# 2026 Great-House Criminal Jurisdiction Register

**STATUS: BROAD-CORE TERRITORIAL CANON, corrected 17 October 2026: Québec to de Villiers.** Created at the user's direction for the **eight Great Houses with developed dossiers — not an exhaustive or capped peerage**. A House's recognized core establishes its **exclusive primary authority over organized criminal enterprises within that core**, subject to expressly recorded border exceptions and unresolved claims. These are broad fictional secret-Order jurisdictions, **not modern public governmental sovereignty**, ordinary ownership or color-filled influence-map territory. No surveyed administrative maps, unsupported national monopoly or new Major/Lesser Houses are created.

**Precedence rule:** Recognized territorial Great House → authorized regional Major → authorized local/specialist Lesser or directly attached enterprise → the criminal organization's own hierarchy. The actual chain may skip layers. Every criminal organization operating in recognized House territory must submit, pay tribute and accept superior reserved authority; continued resistance is crushed as an effective organization. Submission preserves substantial operational autonomy, not criminal constitutional rank. [Universal doctrine](house-criminal-vassalage-and-tribute.md).

**Scope policy:** The broad primary cores below are newly established in this register. The words *district*, *core* and *locality* **do not draw exact street boundaries**; border adjudication, charters for unratified Houses, cross-border exceptions and neutral centers remain open. Outside the enumerated cores, a House might have **deep influence without exclusive underworld jurisdiction**. An unresolved locality is not magically ownerless, but no particular claimant is canonized there yet.

## House-by-House Baseline

### Ravenscroft

**Primary territorial core (adopted):** United States (50 states and D.C.), plus western/central Canada through Ontario.

**Senior House/direct centers:** Hudson Valley–New York senior center; U.S. network; Ontario and western Canadian regional administration.

**Delegated jurisdiction:** Existing subordinate Major/Lesser and cadet jurisdictions; senior House can retain direct domains.

**Boundary control and exclusions:** Atlantic Canadian provinces assigned to Wycliffe; Québec belongs to de Villiers; Canada’s far north needs charter detail; overseas influence is not territory.

**Current disposition:** Core primary; Québec de Villiers and northern boundary details open. See the [House dossier](../houses/ravenscroft.html) and the [Powers and Dependency Register](powers-and-dependency-register-2026.md).

### Wycliffe

**Primary territorial core (adopted):** United Kingdom and Atlantic Canada (Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland and Labrador).

**Senior House/direct centers:** Buckinghamshire/Northmere; London–St James’s; Atlantic Canadian legacy Houses and relationships.

**Delegated jurisdiction:** Wycliffe cadet and bound offices (specific criminal collection chains require later assignment).

**Boundary control and exclusions:** Québec belongs to de Villiers; Ontario/western Canada Ravenscroft primacy; Commonwealth ties elsewhere not automatic territory.

**Current disposition:** UK/Atlantic Canada primary; Québec de Villiers. See the [House dossier](../houses/wycliffe.html) and the [Powers and Dependency Register](powers-and-dependency-register-2026.md).

### Falkenrath

**Primary territorial core (adopted):** Austria and Bavaria (Germany).

**Senior House/direct centers:** Salzburg-Schloss Falkenrath; Vienna; Munich/Bavaria with Lichtenegg; Tyrol/western Salzburg with Tannberg.

**Delegated jurisdiction:** Existing Lichtenegg and Tannberg cadet mandates; other local implementation not yet named.

**Boundary control and exclusions:** Baden-Württemberg, Czechia, Hungary, Swiss border, Slovenia, northern Italy are influence/negotiation, not blanket Falkenrath jurisdiction; North America Ravenscroft.

**Current disposition:** Austria/Bavaria primary; outer Central Europe open. See the [House dossier](../houses/falkenrath.html) and the [Powers and Dependency Register](powers-and-dependency-register-2026.md).

### Veyrholm

**Primary territorial core (adopted):** Sweden and Denmark; southern/western Finnish coastal core (Helsinki/Turku); Oslo and key Norwegian coastal nodes; Tallinn/Reval.

**Senior House/direct centers:** Gotland Keep and Stockholm; Kalmar, Øresund, Åbo and Reval branch centers.

**Delegated jurisdiction:** Four established major blood-cadet lines: Kalmar, Øresund, Åbo, Reval; specific Lesser chains as separately approved.

**Boundary control and exclusions:** Norwegian interior, inland Finland, rest of Estonia, Riga, Lithuania/Vilnius, Gdańsk and northern Germany are not automatically Veyrholm primary merely because of maritime reach.

**Current disposition:** Nordic and Tallinn coastal core primary; Baltic/Finland/Norway interior demarcations open. See the [House dossier](../houses/veyrholm.html) and the [Powers and Dependency Register](powers-and-dependency-register-2026.md).

### Volkov

**Primary territorial core (adopted):** Western and central Ukraine (Lviv–Carpathian borderlands through Kyiv); inland Polish House districts centered on Warsaw–Kraków–Lublin; western Belarus borderlands.

**Senior House/direct centers:** Vovchyn Castle; Lviv and Kyiv; Warsaw Volkov House; Kraków senior residences.

**Delegated jurisdiction:** Existing separate Volkov blood/branch and professional nodes; avoid fabricating not-yet-chartered local Majors.

**Boundary control and exclusions:** Not the entirety of Poland, Belarus, Ukraine or western Russia. Vilnius, Baltic coastal cities and Gdańsk are unsettled Volkov–Veyrholm or other jurisdictions.

**Current disposition:** Listed Ukrainian/Polish core primary; Belarus and Baltic lines open. See the [House dossier](../houses/volkov.html) and the [Powers and Dependency Register](powers-and-dependency-register-2026.md).

### de Villiers

**Primary territorial core (adopted):** Québec (Canada); mainland France; approved de Bréval Belgian urban charter districts around Brussels, Liège and Antwerp; selected de Corvelles-linked Romand localities.

**Senior House/direct centers:** Burgundian Montrevaux and Paris; Loire, Provence, Northern France, Alpine/Rhône, Belgian and Suisse-romande Majors.

**Delegated jurisdiction:** Six locked direct Major Houses and their approved Belgian/Swiss/Monaco/Luxembourg Lesser Houses.

**Boundary control and exclusions:** Geneva is multi-House—not a de Villiers citywide criminal monopoly. Full Belgium outside confirmed charter districts, Luxembourg and Monaco citywide, and non-Romand Switzerland remain unresolved. Specialist Lessers do not transfer foreign national sovereignty.

**Current disposition:** France and selected Belgian/Romand charter districts primary; international enclaves open. See the [House dossier](../houses/de-villiers.html) and the [Powers and Dependency Register](powers-and-dependency-register-2026.md).

### Valdieri

**Primary territorial core (adopted):** Piedmont and Liguria, Italy; Rocca delle Fonti principal seat; Milan senior-held properties (not entire Milan).

**Senior House/direct centers:** Gesso/Valdieri; Cuneo; Turin–Canavese/Ivrea; Genoa/west and east Liguria; direct Milan seat.

**Delegated jurisdiction:** Four LOCKED Major Houses: di Genova, di Canavese, Roccabruna and Marenesi; direct and Major-subordinate approved Lesser Houses.

**Boundary control and exclusions:** Wider Lombardy, all Milan outside its actual senior enclave, rest of Italy, Swiss/French borderlands remain open; Falkenrath investment in north Italy does not displace Piedmont/Liguria local primacy.

**Current disposition:** Named Peruvian and Bolivian primary cores; details/other regions open. See the [House dossier](../houses/amaru.html) and the [Powers and Dependency Register](powers-and-dependency-register-2026.md).

## Priority territorial adjudications (intentionally not settled)

1. **Canada:** **de Villiers holds Québec as its primary territorial criminal jurisdiction**. The supplied North American atlas's historical label *de Varenne* is an obsolete out-of-universe working name, replaced by de Villiers, not an in-world House predecessor. Ravenscroft is primary throughout the United States and in western/central Canada through Ontario; Wycliffe is primary in Atlantic Canada. Both can retain substantial Québec influence, but neither displaces de Villiers' jurisdiction.
2. **Baltic and Polish coast:** Veyrholm coastal-maritime reach and Volkov overland/Polish continuity overlap near Riga, Vilnius, Klaipėda, Gdańsk and Baltic routes. A port contract is not a proprietary criminal territory; exact primary territorial claims here still require review.
3. **Alpine/Rhine interfaces:** Austria/Bavaria belong to Falkenrath; Piedmont/Liguria to Valdieri; mainland France to de Villiers. Northern Italy, Switzerland (especially Geneva), southwestern Germany and some Alpine approaches need specific carve-outs rather than overlaying three Great-House shades.
4. **Belgium/Suisse romande/Monaco/Luxembourg:** de Villiers' approved subordinate-House charters establish compelling home-network responsibility, but specialist lesser-house presence does not automatically establish every street or all of another state as exclusive criminal territory.
5. **Andean cross-border reach:** Amaru has enumerated highland, commercial and capital districts; Ecuador, Chile, other Peruvian/Bolivian regions and broad influence-map shading are not automatically Amaru underworld jurisdictions.
6. **Undeveloped Great Houses:** No territory is assigned here to Ashcroft, Shen, Kuroda, atlas-referenced Xolotl or other Houses whose territorial dossiers are incomplete. The number of Great Houses is **open-ended**; the nine Council mandates do not cap the peerage. House Xolotl appears in the user-supplied North America atlas but requires separate dossier and constitutional reconciliation. Their existing or future sovereign rights are reserved; this register is **not a worldwide partition of the Earth**.

## How the register is to be used

When a specific fictional syndicate is introduced, identify **its operating locality first**. Consult the territorial core above, then any explicitly confirmed Major/Lesser delegated district and any actual inter-House treaty. If its district is unresolved, mark the criminal jurisdiction **disputed/unassigned**, not a house guessed from neighboring map colors. A multi-regional enterprise can have one syndicate chief but distinct local House obligations. The criminal enterprise **does not become the beneficial property** of the Great House merely because it owes tribute.
