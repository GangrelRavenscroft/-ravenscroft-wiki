# 2026 Great-House Wealth — Ownership Scenarios

**DEVELOPMENT NOTES, NOT LOCKED ASSETS.** This document sketches how the proposed principal-House net-worth ranges could be supported by plausible, diverse beneficial ownership. It does not identify actual present-day corporations as owned by fictional Houses. All numbers are illustrative USD billions of **net beneficial equity**, not gross enterprise value, revenue, assets under management, borrowed capital, or assets owned separately by cadets. Samples are mutually exclusive allocation categories **within each House**.

The sample figures are **single illustrative midpoints, not independently approved asset inventories**, and should never be cited as fixed property values in a biography or House page. Actual finalized structures require names of holding vehicles, asset categories and how House trusts are the beneficial owners. A trust holding shares already listed in another category is **not** an additional asset.

## House-specific validation before committing any number

### Wycliffe

The enormous financial footprint must be broken into **assets actually owned** and **client money merely advised or administered**. A trust company managing $1T of outside capital is not a $1T Wycliffe family asset. Wycliffe may be influential in governments, courts and financial institutions without owning them. Distinct multi-century portfolio investments, genuine equity in financial companies, property and infrastructure can support its scale.

- **What requires design:** actual owned financial platforms and major unrelated equity holdings; trust beneficial ownership, cross-border institutional relationships and consolidated debt.
- **What must remain intact:** established family, Northmere estate, British/CW institutional history, House offices and historic core.

### Veyrholm

It has no singular "Veyrholm Corporation". Instead, multiple distinct maritime, insurance, finance, communication and private capital companies may be held through trusts and partnerships, each with a different ownership and management structure. Some operate across Baltic, Nordic and international markets; do not imply monopoly of world shipping.

- **What requires design:** relative beneficial stakes versus partnered port interests, ownership of investable insurance capital versus policyholder assets, and precise cadet ownership boundaries.
- **What must remain intact:** established maritime and logistics institutions, historic 1689 cadet relationship, Gotland history and no single mega-company.

### Falkenrath

The already-established **Falkenrath Werke Holding AG** remains approximately **€22–27B in annual revenue, 55,000–65,000 workers and €30–35B in enterprise value**, with **72% controlled by protected dynastic vehicles**. Enterprise value is **not equity value**; exact House economic equity may be below 72% of enterprise value depending on debt and holdings. The illustrative **$16B exposure** is just a working placeholder pending that debt reconciliation.

A $800B overall House fortune would necessarily come **mostly from many other valuable businesses, diversified investments, infrastructure and estates** beyond Falkenrath Werke. Those additional ownership vehicles still need to be built convincingly. Merely asserting €35B Falkenrath Werke is somehow worth $800B would violate canon.

- **What requires design:** long-duration accumulated investments, other businesses, trust boundaries, industrial equity holdings and debt.
- **What must remain intact:** Works revenue/employees/EV, its board governance, 1957 Articles and Central-European industrial character.

### Volkov

Its long history of territorial loss and rebuilding creates a preference for dispersion. A $700B principal fortune should emerge from legally distinguishable holdings in multiple jurisdictions, different strategic sectors and independent financial entities, not a mysterious undifferentiated "black fund" or one mythical Volkov conglomerate.

- **What requires design:** succession-protected beneficial ownership across jurisdictions, productive finance and logistics assets, liquidity during war and sanctions, and limits on accessible capital.
- **What must remain intact:** Volkov Continuity Trust's coordination function, strong geographic dispersion, cross-border history, named locations and key House personnel.

## What has NOT been assigned

- New names, boards, specific operating-company acquisitions or actual control of real listed corporations.
- Individual family or cadet-house equity shares.
- Balance sheets, debt schedules or marked-to-market values for particular institutions.
- A claim that broad institutional influence is equivalent to ownership.

## Why Valdieri stays weak

The **proposed** $3.5–5B for the principal Valdieri Great House is a genuine net worth, excluding all independent **Genova** and **Canavese** assets. Its larger network of subordinate Houses, industry, law and patronage can exceed its own owned assets without changing the net worth figure. Recent restoration and heavy continuity costs prevent it from matching the older Houses despite formal constitutional equality.

## Recommended next round

**Approve wealth ranges** before canonizing them. Then develop one House at a time: map actual beneficial ownership to named financial and operating institutions, identify House-common versus senior private wealth, allocate cadet properties independently, and create a nonadditive influence-assets table.
