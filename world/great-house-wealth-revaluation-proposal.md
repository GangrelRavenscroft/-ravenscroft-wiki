## Required definitions

- **Principal Great-House net worth:** Net beneficially owned assets of the senior/ruling family and genuinely House-common sovereign trusts or property. Do **not** add separately owned cadet Houses, bound Major and Lesser Houses, independent allies, patronees or third-party property.
- **Cadet House net worth:** A separate balance sheet for each chartered subordinate cadet House; constitutional subordination is not ownership by the Great-House Head.
- **Controlled or influence-bearing assets:** Distinct, overlapping value of firms, managed investments, finance arrangements and institutional assets over which the House has meaningful control or influence. They are **not additional personal wealth** and must not be added to net worth.
- **Political, technological, criminal, industrial and information reach:** Different measures of power, often not meaningfully valued in cash.
- Deduplicate interlocking holdings, common trusts, joint family property, beneficially owned corporate equity, managed third-party assets and enterprise values. Record whether wealth is public, hidden, liquid, pledged or protected.

Most Great Houses in this setting should be as wealthy as, or wealthier than, the richest families in ordinary contemporary life. They also have much greater reach through layered institutions and subordinate Houses. **Valdieri is the exceptional newly restored and weakest Great House**.
