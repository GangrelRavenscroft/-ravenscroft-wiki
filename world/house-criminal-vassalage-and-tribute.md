# Territorial Criminal Supremacy, Tribute and Enforcement

**WORLD-WIDE CANON — approved 17 October 2026.** Latest user clarification **supersedes earlier affiliation-only doctrine**. [See world power structure](great-house-power-structure.html#territorial-criminal-jurisdiction).

## Compulsory submission

**Every Great House has ultimate authority over organized criminal enterprises operating within its recognized hidden House territory.** Those enterprises are required to submit, respect the House's reserved authority and pay imposed tribute, even if they did not previously accept an affiliation. A criminal syndicate has no recognized right to remain fully independent in a Great House's jurisdiction by declining House protection. This rule holds for **all** recognized Great Houses and is not an Amaru exception.

A Great House's **recognized criminal jurisdiction** is distinct from modern national sovereignty, civil property rights and a broader, often overlapping, commercial or political influence map. Territorial districts may be personally administered by the senior House or delegated to its Major and Lesser Houses. Actual boundaries, enclaves, arrangements and neutral areas require specific House canon and are not generated from atlas colors.

## Semi-autonomy, delegated command and tribute

A group that submits remains a **semi-autonomous criminal vassal**. Its chief, internal hierarchy, subordinate groups, businesses and substantial daily operating freedom remain intact unless its superior legitimately intervenes. The immediate authority may be a Great House, a Major House, a Lesser House, or another criminal organization. Crime hierarchies and dynastic ranks remain separate.

**Required tribute and other obligations filter up to the sovereign Great House.** The intermediary Houses and criminal groups may keep the shares allowed within the hierarchy, but the required senior portion reaches the Great House. No universal rate, precise payment method, annual revenue or specific syndicate is established. Underworld tribute is not lawful public taxation. It is not correct financial accounting to count the same sum repeatedly as it ascends multiple tiers, or to treat a criminal group's entire turnover as House net worth.

## Refusal, punishment and destruction

An organization that does not submit is **defiant within the territorial system**, not an equal independent criminal polity. It can be punished, and **continuing resistance leads the Great House to crush it as a functioning autonomous organization**. This does not require killing every member.

Great Houses possess extraordinary manpower, intelligence, finances and security resources. Their fictional enforcement repertoire ranges from removal of patronage, restrictions and leadership replacement to targeted killings/assassinations, conflicts involving House-loyal criminal groups and direct House intervention. These are broad narrative possibilities, not tactical instructions or approval of any individual incident. Retaliation, innocent casualties, reputation, legal exposure and Compact/inter-House repercussions can make such coercion costly. A Great House is overwhelmingly powerful relative to most syndicates, not magically invulnerable.

## Jurisdiction and cross-border conflicts

The local **territorial Great House** has priority over criminal organizations working inside its recognized domain, even if a syndicate's leadership or foreign patrons owe loyalty to another House. Activities across two or more Great-House jurisdictions encounter each House's territorial claims; possible special agreements must be specifically established. Contested territory may lead to rival claims resolved by negotiation or Order channels. Do not invent exact borders or guaranteed payment arrangements.

An enterprise can be **independently owned and managed** but still owe territorial allegiance and tribute. Outside recognized Great-House territory or amid unsettled claims a group might remain genuinely independent. Within recognized territory a noncompliant group may operate covertly for a time, but it is resisting rather than permanently exempt.

The Veyr criminal-business network keeps its existing distinct family and business identity and is **not automatically de Villiers property** because of marriage; its criminal operations nevertheless owe territorial compliance wherever they take place. Specific agreements, jurisdiction and tribute remain unassigned.

## Knowledge, narrative and canon safety

Low-ranking gang members may know only the next immediate criminal superior, while syndicate leaders know a Major or Lesser sponsor without recognizing the apex Great House or Order. Submission, criminal affiliation, House membership, bloodline, financial ownership, Order induction and clearance are all different.

Do not invent named criminal enterprises, gang wars, assassinations, specific commands, tribute percentages, routes, fiscal receipts, exact boundaries or new constitutional House titles from the doctrine alone. A Great House's jurisdiction does not mean it owns public governments, innocent citizens or all ordinary businesses.

**House Amaru:** Andean drug-trade access is acknowledged. Criminal organizations within Amaru's *recognized House territory* must submit; the broader Peru–Bolivia influence sphere is not automatically an exclusive contiguous criminal jurisdiction. No particular syndicate, territorial line or crime incident is added. Approved 2026 principal net wealth **US$350–650B** and nonadditive sphere of influenced/controlled assets **US$1–2T** remain intact.
