# Criminal Vassalage and Tribute in the House System

**WORLD CANON — Approved by the user, 17 October 2026.** Companion to the [Great-House Power Structure](great-house-power-structure.html#criminal-vassalage-and-tribute).

## The essential distinction

A criminal enterprise **bound** into a Great House's network is a **semi-autonomous vassal**. It independently administers ordinary operations, leaders, subordinate groups and a meaningful share of profits, but it owes obedience, payments and compliance with superior reserved decisions. **Ultimate authority resides with the Great House**, regardless of whether the immediate superior is that Great House, a chartered Major House, a Lesser House, or a more powerful criminal enterprise. Criminal organizations retain internal hierarchies separate from the Great/Major/Lesser constitution and never become constitutional Houses merely by affiliation.

**Independent, allied, transactional and rival groups remain possible.** A mere commercial or criminal contact is not automatically bound, even within a recognized Great House's regional influence. The Veyr Network's existing fictional independent status cannot be overwritten by a general rule.

## Territorial and criminal chains

Example only: **Great House → Major House → Lesser House → organized-crime syndicate → local criminal organization → street gang.** Actual chains can be shorter, longer or bypass intermediate tiers. A Great House may directly oversee criminal enterprises; a Major or Lesser may do likewise; a powerful syndicate may govern smaller groups independently within its own chartered obligations. This does not require assigning every local territory to a criminal organization.

## Tribute and the upward flow

Subordinates pay periodic **tribute, taxes in the underworld sense, or other agreed obligations** up to their immediate superior. An intermediate House or enterprise may retain a share, sending the required amount onward; ultimately part of the obligations and financial value reach the senior Great House. **No universal percentage, payment channel, criminal revenue estimate or collection process is invented.** A single payment moving through successive hands cannot count multiple times as House-owned net worth. Criminal turnover, annual income, accumulated equity, public assets and political influence must not be conflated. A Great House's *capacity* to demand tribute does not mean every regional criminal group pays it.

## Credible enforcement and the costs of violence

Great Houses possess extraordinary capital, intelligence, security and manpower relative to most affiliated criminal organizations. Their superior power permits serious discipline of disobedient enterprises. Consequences can include withdrawal of patronage and protection, loss of status, reorganization, removal of leaders and, in severe fictional conflicts, **assassination, conflict involving loyal rival underworld organizations, and direct House intervention**.

Those consequences are possibilities for the setting, **not prescriptions, methods or automatic outcomes**. Violence, reprisal cycles, exposure, collateral harm, criminal investigations, counterpressure from another Great House, and potential Compact consequences can make an intervention costly or politically disastrous. House authority is real and often decisive, not infallible or omnipotent.

## Knowledge and secrecy

A local criminal actor may know only the first superior. A syndicate leadership may be aware of a Lesser or Major House patron without knowing the Great-House apex, while senior figures may understand part of the hierarchy without knowing the Order's inner affairs. **Tribute does not confer House membership, dynastic blood privileges, Order induction or security clearance.** The knowledge chain is distinct from the actual command chain.

## Canon-control requirements

For every specifically named criminal group, distinguish actual operational superior, House superior (if any), whether allegiance is binding, territorial/specialist remit, tribute obligation, actual retained benefit, who knows which superior, internal agency, and conflicts of interest. **Do not invent affiliated syndicates, local gang names, specific assassinations or wars as consequences of a general world-wide rule alone.**

**House Amaru:** The user favors meaningful access to Andean drug-trade networks; its precise group affiliations, participating regional Houses, numerical revenue/tribute and individual incidents remain undetermined. The now-approved principal $350–650B and nonadditive influence $1–2T bands do not assume unspecified illicit proceeds as audited capital.
