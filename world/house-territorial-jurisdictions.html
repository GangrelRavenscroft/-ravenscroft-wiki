Broad-Core Territorial Assignments · October 17, 2026

Influence extends. Jurisdiction governs.

Recognized territorial Great Houses hold compulsory criminal authority inside their House jurisdictions. Subordinate syndicates retain everyday autonomy but owe tribute up the layered hierarchy. These cores are more precise than geographic influence zones—yet exact borders and contested cities still require separate charters.

Eight developed Great Houses are included. This is a fictional hidden-Order map of administration and criminal primacy, not public sovereignty, state ownership, or a claim that governments and citizens belong to dynasties. The underworld categories below describe fictional ecosystems, not named criminal companies or particular offenses by House members.

Territory ≠ influence ≠ ownership. A House's larger investment or intelligence footprint cannot automatically take criminal jurisdiction from another territorial House. Precise unresolved claims remain explicitly open.

What Power Depends On

The House is legally sovereign within the hidden dynastic Order but operationally reliant on subordinate families, professional services, foreign counterparties, infrastructure and local legitimacy. 04

Unresolved Boundary Questions

Canada: Quebec (Wycliffe/Ravenscroft). Baltics: Gdańsk, Vilnius, Riga and maritime/land corridors (Veyrholm/Volkov and local charters). Alps: Switzerland, Milan's wider urban environs, and French/Austrian/Italian boundaries (de Villiers/Falkenrath/Valdieri). Andes: territory outside the specifically designated Amaru cores. International cities may need negotiated exceptional mandates.

Untreated territories are reserved for later development—including not-yet-developed Great Houses—and are never awarded simply because an existing House has the strongest shading on an atlas plate.
