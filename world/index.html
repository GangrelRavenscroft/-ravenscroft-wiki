Setting Frame

A contemporary world with an older hidden architecture

Public life remains recognizably modern. Behind it, Great Houses, affiliated dynasties, and the international Order maintain long-lived private systems of governance, patronage, influence, intelligence, education, and security.

Visibility

The Houses are not theatrical secret societies. Their public institutions, businesses, universities, foundations, estates, and careers are real. Hidden power sits behind ordinary legitimacy rather than replacing it.

Technology

House capabilities are somewhat ahead of the public baseline in surveillance, drones, AI-assisted analysis, secure systems, and longevity-related medicine, while remaining contemporary rather than science-fictional.

Geography

Ravenscroft blood family members normally maintain primary residence in the United States. Overseas primary residences exist but are deliberately uncommon; international properties are more often secondary, professional, or family bases.

Formation

Ravenscroft blood members normally encounter initial Order induction around age eighteen and deeper adult formation around age twenty-one. Knowledge, clearance, wealth, blood status, patronage, and operational authority remain separate categories.

Governance

The Order is international and governed above any single House or regional structure. House leadership and Order leadership are distinct offices, even when senior Ravenscrofts hold major Order positions.
