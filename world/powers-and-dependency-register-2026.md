# Powers and Dependency Register — Eight Developed Great Houses, 2026

**STATUS:** Global structural rules and recognized territorial cores are **CANON**; the qualitative dependency and risk assessments below are **analyst interpretation for worldbuilding** unless already substantiated in a House dossier. No extra Great Houses, subordinates, companies or organized-crime affiliates are ratified by this register.

This register is designed to answer: **Who has authority over whom? What can they demand? What do they actually own? Whose cooperation do they need? What breaks when that cooperation fails?**

## I. Powers by hierarchy — binding structural rule

| Level | Constitutional / territorial authority | Financial claim | Actual operating dependence | Hidden knowledge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| International Order / Compact | Common inter-House law, secrecy, adjudication and restraint; cannot casually replace sovereign dynastic governance | Specific dues/remedies only if independently chartered; NOT presumptive ownership of all House assets | Great-House compliance and Council/Conclave legitimacy | Classified by membership/clearance, not wealth alone |
| Sovereign Great House | Supreme dynastic authority over subordinate House network and **organized crime in recognized House territory**; reserved succession and security powers | Beneficially held senior/common assets + actual tribute received; NOT independently owned Major/Lesser estates or entire syndicate turnover | Delegated Major/Lesser Houses, professional institutions, private personnel, geography and Order constraints | Holds the broadest map of territorial and classified dependencies |
| Major House | Regional House administration, directly beneath the Great House; may run its own land, institutions and local underworld-supervisory duties | Retains independently owned property and any authorized income/tribute share | Its own Lesser Houses, local professions and the Great House's reserved recognition | Knows its own remits; may not see all senior secrets |
| Lesser House | Chartered local or specialist authority, directly below Major or Great | Its own separately owned assets and permitted tributary share | Major/senior patron, staff, local stakeholders and often larger criminal enterprises | Limited to warranted duties and authorized access |
| Semi-autonomous criminal syndicate | Runs everyday criminal hierarchy after submission, but owes territorial House obedience; may supervise smaller organizations | Retains profits/assets subject to tribute and other superior obligations | House protection/toleration, local commercial environment, subordinates and rival pressures | May know only immediate House contact, not the apex |
| Local group or gang | Internal lower criminal hierarchy; no dynastic House rank | Retained earnings after obligations | Larger syndicate or supervising intermediary; locally exposed to House coercion | Often unaware of Great House or Order |

**Jurisdiction precedence:** **local recognized territorial Great House** > its authorized Major/Lesser supervisory chain > the operating criminal chain. Cross-House agreements can modify the relationship; unresolved boundaries remain unresolved, not a unilateral foreign override.

**Transfer accounting:** Every payment is recorded **once as a flow**, with only the ultimate retained economic shares booked to the appropriate beneficiaries. Five successive tribute handoffs do not multiply Great-House net worth. Independently owned subordinate property, managed client capital and inferred political leverage are not principal-House beneficial equity.

## III. House operating profiles

### Ravenscroft

**Sovereign territorial power:** United States (50 states and D.C.), plus western/central Canada through Ontario.

**Power levers:** U.S.-anchored strategic capital, superior intelligence/technology, financial institutions and high-capacity security.

**Who implements local authority:** Existing subordinate Major/Lesser and cadet jurisdictions; senior House can retain direct domains.

**Internal and external dependencies:** Delegated local House knowledge, trusted public-facing institutions, legitimate North American logistics, Canadian boundary accords.

**Principal pressure/failure mode:** Vast reporting span; reliance on delegated administration; de Villiers Québec territorial interface / Atlantic Canada separation.

**Recognized power limits:** Atlantic Canadian provinces assigned to Wycliffe; Québec belongs to de Villiers; Canada’s far north needs charter detail; overseas influence is not territory.

**Financial scale:** Principal only: unassigned; strategic primacy locked. These figures, where listed, are principal beneficial net worth vs nonadditive influence assets. They do not count criminal turnover as family property.

**Register status:** Core primary; Québec de Villiers and northern boundary details open.

### Wycliffe

**Sovereign territorial power:** United Kingdom and Atlantic Canada (Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland and Labrador).

**Power levers:** Trusts, finance, institutional law, diplomatic precedent, intelligence and extremely durable wealth.

**Who implements local authority:** Wycliffe cadet and bound offices (specific criminal collection chains require later assignment).

**Internal and external dependencies:** Independent fiduciaries and capital markets; London professional networks; Canadian local loyalty and negotiated Raven boundary.

**Principal pressure/failure mode:** Public transparency, fiduciary and reputational exposure; de Villiers Québec jurisdiction.

**Recognized power limits:** Québec belongs to de Villiers; Ontario/western Canada Ravenscroft primacy; Commonwealth ties elsewhere not automatic territory.

**Financial scale:** $850B–1.25T principal; $3–5T nonadditive reach. These figures, where listed, are principal beneficial net worth vs nonadditive influence assets. They do not count criminal turnover as family property.

**Register status:** UK/Atlantic Canada primary; Québec de Villiers.

### Falkenrath

**Sovereign territorial power:** Austria and Bavaria (Germany).

**Power levers:** Industrial companies, Alpine transport expertise, institutional archives, protected capital and credible coercive capacity.

**Who implements local authority:** Existing Lichtenegg and Tannberg cadet mandates; other local implementation not yet named.

**Internal and external dependencies:** Specialist technical personnel; manufacturing suppliers; energy and rail systems; cadet/regional consent.

**Principal pressure/failure mode:** Industrial disruption, corporate exposure, contested Alpine approaches.

**Recognized power limits:** Baden-Württemberg, Czechia, Hungary, Swiss border, Slovenia, northern Italy are influence/negotiation, not blanket Falkenrath jurisdiction; North America Ravenscroft.

**Financial scale:** $650–950B principal; $2–3.5T nonadditive reach. These figures, where listed, are principal beneficial net worth vs nonadditive influence assets. They do not count criminal turnover as family property.

**Register status:** Austria/Bavaria primary; outer Central Europe open.

### Veyrholm

**Sovereign territorial power:** Sweden and Denmark; southern/western Finnish coastal core (Helsinki/Turku); Oslo and key Norwegian coastal nodes; Tallinn/Reval.

**Power levers:** Maritime transport, port relationships, communications, insurance/finance and rerouting expertise.

**Who implements local authority:** Four established major blood-cadet lines: Kalmar, Øresund, Åbo, Reval; specific Lesser chains as separately approved.

**Internal and external dependencies:** Independent port operators and shipowners, labor and shipping networks, coastal institutions, Baltic negotiated access.

**Principal pressure/failure mode:** Shipping chokepoints; Volkov overlap at Baltic ports and land corridors.

**Recognized power limits:** Norwegian interior, inland Finland, rest of Estonia, Riga, Lithuania/Vilnius, Gdańsk and northern Germany are not automatically Veyrholm primary merely because of maritime reach.

**Financial scale:** $750B–1.1T principal; $2.5–4T nonadditive reach. These figures, where listed, are principal beneficial net worth vs nonadditive influence assets. They do not count criminal turnover as family property.

**Register status:** Nordic and Tallinn coastal core primary; Baltic/Finland/Norway interior demarcations open.

### Volkov

**Sovereign territorial power:** Western and central Ukraine (Lviv–Carpathian borderlands through Kyiv); inland Polish House districts centered on Warsaw–Kraków–Lublin; western Belarus borderlands.

**Power levers:** Distributed continuity archives, borderland ties, dispersed reserve structures and overland resilience.

**Who implements local authority:** Existing separate Volkov blood/branch and professional nodes; avoid fabricating not-yet-chartered local Majors.

**Internal and external dependencies:** Rail, road and borderland contacts; cross-border lawful ownership; protected archives; independent Polish/Ukrainian professional partners.

**Principal pressure/failure mode:** War/displacement, sanctions and trade disruption; Baltic territorial disputes.

**Recognized power limits:** Not the entirety of Poland, Belarus, Ukraine or western Russia. Vilnius, Baltic coastal cities and Gdańsk are unsettled Volkov–Veyrholm or other jurisdictions.

**Financial scale:** $550–850B principal; $1.5–3T nonadditive reach. These figures, where listed, are principal beneficial net worth vs nonadditive influence assets. They do not count criminal turnover as family property.

**Register status:** Listed Ukrainian/Polish core primary; Belarus and Baltic lines open.

### de Villiers

**Sovereign territorial power:** Québec (Canada); mainland France; approved de Bréval Belgian urban charter districts around Brussels, Liège and Antwerp; selected de Corvelles-linked Romand localities.

**Power levers:** Civil engineering and strategic works, cross-border professional networks, technically competent six-Major constitution.

**Who implements local authority:** Six locked direct Major Houses and their approved Belgian/Swiss/Monaco/Luxembourg Lesser Houses.

**Internal and external dependencies:** Lawful contracts and licensed engineers, public concession counterparties, legal-financial frameworks, Belgian/Swiss local charters.

**Principal pressure/failure mode:** Neutral Swiss/diplomatic centers; inter-jurisdictional compliance and reputational risk.

**Recognized power limits:** Geneva is multi-House—not a de Villiers citywide criminal monopoly. Full Belgium outside confirmed charter districts, Luxembourg and Monaco citywide, and non-Romand Switzerland remain unresolved. Specialist Lessers do not transfer foreign national sovereignty.

**Financial scale:** Principal wealth not locked; $760B–1.15T proposed only. These figures, where listed, are principal beneficial net worth vs nonadditive influence assets. They do not count criminal turnover as family property.

**Register status:** France and selected Belgian/Romand charter districts primary; international enclaves open.

### Valdieri

**Sovereign territorial power:** Piedmont and Liguria, Italy; Rocca delle Fonti principal seat; Milan senior-held properties (not entire Milan).

**Power levers:** Restored sovereign status, technical competence, strong regional charters and archival proof of obligations.

**Who implements local authority:** Four LOCKED Major Houses: di Genova, di Canavese, Roccabruna and Marenesi; direct and Major-subordinate approved Lesser Houses.

**Internal and external dependencies:** Separate wealth and personnel of four Majors; professional partners and capital reserves; external capital and inter-House cooperation.

**Principal pressure/failure mode:** Senior Great House relatively cash-poor versus peers; vulnerability to powerful financiers and loss of cadet cooperation.

**Recognized power limits:** Wider Lombardy, all Milan outside its actual senior enclave, rest of Italy, Swiss/French borderlands remain open; Falkenrath investment in north Italy does not displace Piedmont/Liguria local primacy.

**Financial scale:** $3.5–5B principal; wider reach unassigned. These figures, where listed, are principal beneficial net worth vs nonadditive influence assets. They do not count criminal turnover as family property.

**Register status:** Piedmont/Liguria primary; Milan building enclave only.

### Amaru

**Sovereign territorial power:** Peruvian Cusco/Sacred Valley–Urubamba, Puno, Arequipa and Lima–Callao districts; Bolivian La Paz–El Alto and western Altiplano core.

**Power levers:** Andean regional knowledge, cross-border political and professional relationships, deep local loyalty and global investment capital.

**Who implements local authority:** Existing Huamán, Valcárcel, Arce, Rojas and four Lesser candidates remain PROPOSED; no automatic ratification; senior House retains primary responsibility pending individual charters.

**Internal and external dependencies:** Trusted local communities, technically competent intermediaries, logistics and ports, regional House cooperation and global lawful finance.

**Principal pressure/failure mode:** Criminal drug networks impose exposure and moral/political liabilities; environmental conflict and difficult terrain.

**Recognized power limits:** Neither Peru nor Bolivia as complete public states, nor Chile, Ecuador, Brazil or Amazonian interior, is automatically exclusive Amaru territory. Individual corridor limits remain open.

**Financial scale:** $350–650B principal; $1–2T nonadditive reach. These figures, where listed, are principal beneficial net worth vs nonadditive influence assets. They do not count criminal turnover as family property.

**Register status:** Named Peruvian and Bolivian primary cores; details/other regions open.

## V. Controlled terms for future lore entries

Use the following field names in any new House or criminal dossier:

- **Territorial House:** Which Great House has recognized primary underworld jurisdiction?
- **Territorial status:** confirmed primary / disputed / shared by ratified treaty / undelimited / foreign influence only.
- **House chain:** great → major → lesser, omitting absent intermediate levels.
- **Criminal chain:** syndicate → regional organization → local group/gang, only where those entities are actually established.
- **Relationship state:** submitted / in negotiations but bound to submit / defiant / actively contested jurisdiction.
- **Ownership:** beneficial equity and independent physical property, NOT authority or influence.
- **Tribute:** obligation, immediate recipient, retained share and ultimate beneficiary; don't invent rates or double count.
- **Security/force:** available capacity, institutional constraints and costs. No operational violence methods.
- **Knowledge:** which actors know their superior, which know the Great House, and which are Order inducted/cleared.
- **Dependencies:** nonowned assets, professional relationships, ports/routes, legal regimes, cadet loyalties and other Houses without whose cooperation this node is degraded.
- **Canon confidence:** locked region, confirmed subordinate charter, inferred dependence, proposed/unknown detail.

## VI. Open register issues

Exact lines within Québec's local charters, Baltic ports, Switzerland/Geneva, Belgian municipalities, Alpine borderlands, German non-Bavarian regions, Andean corridors, portions of Ukraine/Poland, Canadian far north and other undeveloped Great-House domains remain **undelimited**. This does not revoke established Great-House claims in the cores above. It prevents the wrong House from receiving exclusive jurisdiction solely from a broad influence map.
