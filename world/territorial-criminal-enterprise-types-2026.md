These are **fictional underworld ecosystem categories**, not proof a named cartel, gang or existing real firm is criminal, not lists of criminal groups the family owns, and not evidence any particular House Head ordered crimes. All groups within a recognized territory must submit; persistent challengers are crushed as functioning organizations. Avoid adding procedural criminal techniques or specific routes.

## Internal organization, not compulsory ownership

A big criminal enterprise might oversee regional organizations and multiple street groups; its own structure remains separate from the Great/Major/Lesser House hierarchy. A House can directly supervise a dominant enterprise or delegate supervision through Major and Lesser Houses. **Who actually owns**, **who commands**, **who benefits financially**, **who receives tribute**, and **who knows the Order exists** are five separate inquiries.

The category list is about **what types occur or matter** in the territory, not a declaration that every House operates or approves each crime. There can be many competing criminal enterprises of one type, and a single enterprise can span multiple types. Avoid treating generic category labels as an invented charter, ownership contract or historical incident.

## House-specific reference profiles

### Ravenscroft — principal types

- National and local narcotics networks
- Organized financial fraud and illicit financial brokerage
- Cyber extortion and organized cybercrime
- Interstate cargo theft and contraband
- Human Trafficking
- Prostitution

**Primary context:** U.S.-anchored strategic capital, superior intelligence/technology, financial institutions and high-capacity security. **Territorial limitation:** Atlantic Canadian provinces assigned to Wycliffe; Québec belongs to de Villiers; Canada’s far north needs charter detail; overseas influence is not territory. **Dependency warning:** Delegated local House knowledge, trusted public-facing institutions, legitimate North American logistics, Canadian boundary accords.

### Wycliffe — principal types

- Financial fraud and investment crime
- Art and luxury-property trafficking
- Cross-channel and Atlantic port contraband
- Organized gambling and loan-sharking
- Cyber financial extortion

**Primary context:** Trusts, finance, institutional law, diplomatic precedent, intelligence and extremely durable wealth. **Territorial limitation:** Québec belongs to de Villiers; Ontario/western Canada Ravenscroft primacy; Commonwealth ties elsewhere not automatic territory. **Dependency warning:** Independent fiduciaries and capital markets; London professional networks; Canadian local loyalty and negotiated Raven boundary.

### Falkenrath — principal types

- Industrial design and intellectual-property theft
- High-value industrial freight theft
- Construction and procurement corruption
- Restricted technological contraband
- Organized commercial extortion

**Primary context:** Industrial companies, Alpine transport expertise, institutional archives, protected capital and credible coercive capacity. **Territorial limitation:** Baden-Württemberg, Czechia, Hungary, Swiss border, Slovenia, northern Italy are influence/negotiation, not blanket Falkenrath jurisdiction; North America Ravenscroft. **Dependency warning:** Specialist technical personnel; manufacturing suppliers; energy and rail systems; cadet/regional consent.

### Veyrholm — principal types

- Maritime contraband and port syndicates
- Cargo theft and illicit freight brokerage
- Cyber/data extortion linked to communications
- Restricted-goods cross-Baltic trafficking
- Coastal commercial protection rackets

**Primary context:** Maritime transport, port relationships, communications, insurance/finance and rerouting expertise. **Territorial limitation:** Norwegian interior, inland Finland, rest of Estonia, Riga, Lithuania/Vilnius, Gdańsk and northern Germany are not automatically Veyrholm primary merely because of maritime reach. **Dependency warning:** Independent port operators and shipowners, labor and shipping networks, coastal institutions, Baltic negotiated access.

### Volkov — principal types

- Cross-border black-market and contraband groups
- Illicit arms and industrial-supply trafficking
- Organized cyber extortion and financial crime
- Commercial-record/identity fraud
- Protection rackets and freight theft

**Primary context:** Distributed continuity archives, borderland ties, dispersed reserve structures and overland resilience. **Territorial limitation:** Not the entirety of Poland, Belarus, Ukraine or western Russia. Vilnius, Baltic coastal cities and Gdańsk are unsettled Volkov–Veyrholm or other jurisdictions. **Dependency warning:** Rail, road and borderland contacts; cross-border lawful ownership; protected archives; independent Polish/Ukrainian professional partners.

### de Villiers — principal types

- Construction/procurement fraud
- Financial, insurance and trade-fraud networks
- Art/heritage and luxury-asset trafficking
- Cross-border high-value contraband
- Commercial extortion and illicit document brokerage

**Primary context:** Civil engineering and strategic works, cross-border professional networks, technically competent six-Major constitution. **Territorial limitation:** Geneva is multi-House—not a de Villiers citywide criminal monopoly. Full Belgium outside confirmed charter districts, Luxembourg and Monaco citywide, and non-Romand Switzerland remain unresolved. Specialist Lessers do not transfer foreign national sovereignty. **Dependency warning:** Lawful contracts and licensed engineers, public concession counterparties, legal-financial frameworks, Belgian/Swiss local charters.

### Valdieri — principal types

- Precision-industry and industrial cargo theft
- Ligurian maritime contraband and port rackets
- Art, archive and provenance crime
- Industrial/construction procurement fraud
- Commercial extortion and loan-sharking

**Primary context:** Restored sovereign status, technical competence, strong regional charters and archival proof of obligations. **Territorial limitation:** Wider Lombardy, all Milan outside its actual senior enclave, rest of Italy, Swiss/French borderlands remain open; Falkenrath investment in north Italy does not displace Piedmont/Liguria local primacy. **Dependency warning:** Separate wealth and personnel of four Majors; professional partners and capital reserves; external capital and inter-House cooperation.

### Amaru — principal types

- Cocaine and other narcotics syndicates
- Illegal mineral extraction and mineral contraband
- Highland cargo theft and transport extortion
- Illegal timber/wildlife trading groups where present
- Cross-border counterfeit and illicit-goods brokerage

**Primary context:** Andean regional knowledge, cross-border political and professional relationships, deep local loyalty and global investment capital. **Territorial limitation:** Neither Peru nor Bolivia as complete public states, nor Chile, Ecuador, Brazil or Amazonian interior, is automatically exclusive Amaru territory. Individual corridor limits remain open. **Dependency warning:** Trusted local communities, technically competent intermediaries, logistics and ports, regional House cooperation and global lawful finance.


**Ravenscroft scope clarification:** Human trafficking and prostitution-related criminal enterprises are distinct categories. Prostitution is not automatically trafficking; the criminal category concerns exploitative, coercive, or otherwise unlawful organized enterprises under applicable law, not all consensual adult sex work. These categories identify underworld sectors within fictional Ravenscroft territory; they do not automatically establish that named Ravenscroft family members personally operate them.

## Drug-trade distinction

**Amaru:** Drug trafficking is a prominent regional organized-crime category because the user's established House concept includes meaningful access to Andean criminal networks. It is not synonymous with all coca-growing communities, many of which participate in legitimate traditional/legal markets. Never assert House title to an entire drug industry or fabricate financial receipts from market size. 

**Other Great Houses:** Their territories may include narcotics networks too even when another form of criminal enterprise provides a more distinctive dramatic focus. Excluding narcotics from a five-category shortlist does not exempt narcotics groups from the local Great House's compulsory authority.

## Boundaries

Recognized **local territorial authority** follows the [Jurisdiction Register](great-house-jurisdiction-register-2026.md); more extensive financial and cultural influence alone does not automatically create criminal jurisdiction abroad. Tribute and any coercive penalties remain under the [universal canon](house-criminal-vassalage-and-tribute.md). No named gang, transaction, assassination, drug route, leader or specific incident is created by this directory.
